# Shaping guarantees

## Purpose
What the brainstorming session does as it shapes items with the person: it drafts and readies lines and asks nothing
back.

## Behaviour

### [g1] Nothing waits on the person's reading
- [r1] When the brainstorming session readies an item, the brainstorming session shall not ask a question.

### [g2] Lines are drafted and readied
- [r2] When the brainstorming session shapes an item, the brainstorming session shall draft the item and ready the item.

### [g3] A quote keeps its turns
- [r3] When the person prompts the brainstorming session, the brainstorming session shall quote a message.

E1|execution (core)|While an assignment is in a batch, when the person merges the proposed change of the batch, the batches service shall fulfil the assignment and set the status of the batch to `merged`.
E2|execution (core)|While an assignment is in a batch, when the person abandons the batch, the batches service shall relinquish the assignment.
E3|execution (core)|When the person opens a batch, the batches service shall claim an assignment.
E4|execution (core)|If the work supply refuses a claim for an assignment, then the batches service shall keep no task of the assignment.
E5|execution (core)|While a task of an assignment is `running`, when the person parks the assignment, the batches service shall defer the assignment.
E6|execution (core)|When the person merges the proposed change of a batch, the batches service shall fulfil every assignment in the batch.
P1|supply (port)|When the batches service claims an assignment, the work supply shall complete the request.
P2|supply (port)|While a batch holds an assignment, if the batches service claims the assignment, then the work supply shall refuse the request.
P3|supply (port)|When the batches service fulfils an assignment, the work supply shall complete the request.
T1|tracking (upstream)|While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar, when a session allocates the item, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `in-batch`.
T2|tracking (upstream)|While an item is `ready` and the item does not meet the readiness bar, if a session allocates the item, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and set the status of the item to `shaped`.
T3|tracking (upstream)|While an item is `in-batch`, when a session discharges the item, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `delivered`.
T4|tracking (upstream)|While an item is `in-batch`, when a session restores the item, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `ready`.

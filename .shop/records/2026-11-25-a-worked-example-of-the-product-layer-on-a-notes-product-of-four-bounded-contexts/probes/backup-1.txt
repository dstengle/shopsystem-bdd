When the person saves the notes, the backup service shall export the notes.
When the person saves the notes, the backup service shall keep a snapshot.
When the notebook service returns a bundle, the backup service shall store a snapshot that has the bundle.
When the backup service exports the notes, the notebook service shall return a bundle.
When the storage service completes the request, the backup service shall set the status of the snapshot to `stored`.
If the storage service refuses the request, then the backup service shall keep the request.
When the backup service stores a snapshot, the storage service shall complete the request.
When the backup service fetches a snapshot, the storage service shall return the snapshot.
When the backup service imports a bundle, the notebook service shall complete the request.
When the person restores a snapshot, the backup service shall fetch the snapshot.
When the storage service returns a snapshot, the backup service shall import the bundle of the snapshot.
If the person restores a snapshot whose status is `lost`, then the backup service shall refuse the request and name the snapshot.
When the backup service stores a snapshot, the backup service shall set the status of the snapshot to `stored`.

# The words of notebook, as sharing says them

## Terms

- **the notebook service** (proper noun) → service notebook. What keeps the owner's notes.
- **note** (noun) → object note. One of the owner's notes.
- **show** (verb, action) → operation notebook.show. Forms: shows, showed, shown. Frame: <actor> shows <note>.
- **hide** (verb, action) → operation notebook.hide. Forms: hides, hid, hidden. Frame: <actor> hides <note>.

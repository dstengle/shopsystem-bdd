# Notebook port

## Purpose

What sharing needs of the notebook it reaches.

## Behaviour

### [g1] A note shown is shown
- [r1] When the sharing service shows a note, the notebook service shall complete the request.

### [g2] A note hidden is hidden
- [r2] When the sharing service hides a note, the notebook service shall complete the request.

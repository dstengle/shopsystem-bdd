# The words of storage, as backup says them

## Terms

- **the storage service** (proper noun) → service storage. What keeps snapshots outside the product.
- **store** (verb, action) → operation storage.store. Forms: stores, stored. Frame: <actor> stores <snapshot>.
- **fetch** (verb, action) → operation storage.fetch. Forms: fetches, fetched. Frame: <actor> fetches <snapshot>.

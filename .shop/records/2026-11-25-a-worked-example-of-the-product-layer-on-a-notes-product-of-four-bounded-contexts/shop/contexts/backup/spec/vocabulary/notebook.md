# The words of notebook, as backup says them

## Terms

- **the notebook service** (proper noun) → service notebook. What keeps the person's notes.
- **bundle** (noun) → object bundle. Every kept note taken together.
- **export** (verb, action) → operation notebook.export. Forms: exports, exported. Frame: <actor> exports the notes.
- **import** (verb, action) → operation notebook.import. Forms: imports, imported. Frame: <actor> imports <bundle>.

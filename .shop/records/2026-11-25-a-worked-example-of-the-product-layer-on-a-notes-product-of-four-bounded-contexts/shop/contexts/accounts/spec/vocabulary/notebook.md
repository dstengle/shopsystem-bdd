# The words of notebook, as accounts says them

## Terms

- **the notebook service** (proper noun) → service notebook. What keeps the person's notes within an allowance.
- **entitle** (verb, action) → operation notebook.entitle. Forms: entitles, entitled. Frame: <actor> entitles <subscription>.

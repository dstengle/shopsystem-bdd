# The words of payments, as accounts says them

## Terms

- **the payments service** (proper noun) → service payments. What takes the person's money for a plan.
- **charge** (verb, action) → operation payments.charge. Forms: charges, charged. Frame: <actor> charges <subscription>.
- **refund** (verb, action) → operation payments.refund. Forms: refunds, refunded. Frame: <actor> refunds <subscription>.

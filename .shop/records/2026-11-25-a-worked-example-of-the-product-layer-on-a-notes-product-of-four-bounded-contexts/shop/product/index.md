# Notes

## Purpose

Short notes a person keeps for themselves, finds again by tag, lets a colleague read, keeps safe outside their device
and pays for when they need more room than the free allowance.

## Actors

- **the person** — someone who keeps notes with the product. Called: the person (notebook, backup, accounts), the
  owner (sharing).
- **the colleague** — someone the person lets read a note; they need not keep notes themselves. Called: the colleague
  (sharing).

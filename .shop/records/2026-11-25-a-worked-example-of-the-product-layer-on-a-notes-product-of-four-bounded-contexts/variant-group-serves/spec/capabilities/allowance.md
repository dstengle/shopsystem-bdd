# Allowance

## Purpose

Enlarging and reducing the allowance of notes, as the accounts context asks.

## Behaviour

### [g1] An allowance enlarged is kept
- [r1] When a client enlarges an allowance, the notes service shall keep the allowance.

### [g2] An allowance reduced is kept
- [r2] When a client reduces an allowance, the notes service shall keep the allowance.

# List every checklist with the items left to check

## Notes

List every checklist the person keeps, each with the number of its items still open (left to check); a new operation of the checklists service beside list, which lists one checklist's items. The language has no 'each X with its Y', so one line returns every checklist and another returns, for any checklist, the number of its open items. The person's words in quote-2

## Requirements

### checklists

#### [g12] Browse the checklists
- [r12] When the person browses, the checklists service shall return every checklist.

#### [g13] Browse the items left to check
- [r13] While a checklist exists, when the person browses, the checklists service shall return the number of items of the checklist whose status is `open`.

### checklists model

```model
service checklists
  operation browse()  outcomes: returned → the checklist
```

- **browse** (verb, action) → operation checklists.browse. Forms: browses, browsed. Frame: <actor> browses.

# The stock ledger's words (assumed)

## Terms

- **the ledger service** (proper noun) → service ledger. The legacy stock ledger.
- **the caller** (proper noun) → actor the caller. Whoever calls the ledger: here, the stock adapter.
- **line** (noun) → object line. One product's row of stock in the ledger.
- **code** (attribute) → attribute line.code. The ledger's product code.
- **units** (attribute) → attribute line.units. How many units the line holds.
- **decrement** (verb, action) → operation ledger.decrement. Forms: decrements, decremented. Frame: <actor> decrements <line>.
- **increment** (verb, action) → operation ledger.increment. Forms: increments, incremented. Frame: <actor> increments <line>.

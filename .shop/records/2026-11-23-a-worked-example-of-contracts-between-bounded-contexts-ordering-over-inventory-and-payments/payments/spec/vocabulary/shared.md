# Shared words

## Terms

- **the merchant** (proper noun) → actor the merchant. A seller that takes card payments; another context.
- **the network** (proper noun) → actor the network. The card network that settles or reverses a capture, later.
- **the cardholder** (proper noun) → actor the cardholder. The person whose card was charged.

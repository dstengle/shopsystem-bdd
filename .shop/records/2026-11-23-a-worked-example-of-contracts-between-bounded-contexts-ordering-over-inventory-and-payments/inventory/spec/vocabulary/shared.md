# Shared words

## Terms

- **the client** (proper noun) → actor the client. Another context that holds and releases stock.
- **the stock keeper** (proper noun) → actor the stock keeper. A person who restocks, moves and counts stock.
- **the clock** (proper noun) → actor the clock. Time passing: an allocation not consumed within its limit lapses.

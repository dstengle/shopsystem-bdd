# Create checklists, add items, check items off and list them

## Notes

A small CLI that keeps checklists: the person creates a checklist, adds items to it, checks an item off and lists the items. First item of a new context.

## Requirements

### checklists

narrator: the person
group: service
purpose: Creating checklists, adding items to them, checking items off and listing them.

#### [g1] Create checklists, add items, check items off and list them
- [r1] When the person creates a checklist with a title, the checklists service shall keep the checklist.

#### [g2] A checklist created without a title is refused
- [r2] If the person creates a checklist without a title, then the checklists service shall refuse the request and name the title.

#### [g3] An item added with a text is kept in the checklist
- [r3] When the person adds an item to a checklist with a text, the checklists service shall keep the item in the checklist.

#### [g4] An item added without a text is refused
- [r4] If the person adds an item to a checklist without a text, then the checklists service shall refuse the request and name the text.

#### [g5] Checking an item sets its status to done
- [r5] When the person checks an item, the checklists service shall set the status of the item to `done`.

#### [g6] Listing a checklist returns every item in it
- [r6] When the person lists a checklist, the checklists service shall return every item in the checklist.

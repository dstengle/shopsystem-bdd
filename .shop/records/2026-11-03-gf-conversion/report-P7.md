## Deviations and calls

1. Witness count. Design §14.2 gives `every K` one included instance; here it gives two when the group also names K in
   the plural (`k_Pl`), and a plural K in a response means those included witnesses. This reproduces the spike's G1.
   `every K that is v` adds one excluded witness with the first other value.
2. The typed Steps funs are generated by `features_vocab.modules` (not `spec_typed`) and compiled through
   `spec_grammar`'s cache.
3. Step definitions match by grammar, not regex: a custom pytest-bdd parser asks the Steps grammar for each step's
   (template, kind) after a cheap word check, parsing each text once per process. Regex patterns stay in `rows()` for
   `features vocab` and the tests (~900 lazy-hole regexes would backtrack badly on legacy steps). Legacy module steps
   still take precedence.
4. The handler registry is in `tests/scenarios/vocab.py`, aliased in `sys.modules` as `vocab`. `features vocab
   --check` loads the plugin's `vocab.py`.
5. `cmd_generate` and `cmd_vocab` live in `scripts/features` to keep both modules under 300 lines.
6. Generation refuses with exit 1 any construct it has no step for: `shall not` with DoTo/Exit/Commit, Hold with an
   actor subject outside logic-only groups, and other condition atoms. A condition expands only when its Means reads
   "its <attr> is [not] …".
7. U3/U8 checks run only for generated scenarios (those with `@g<n>` marks), via the recording `run` fixture.
8. `conftest.py` removed the 4 pre-existing unused `noqa: E402` comments from `scripts/features`, and is frozen from
   here on.

## Module-map rows

- `scripts/features_generate.py` | `features generate`: a codified capability's groups written as scenarios, instances
  and witnesses by rule (design §14.2)
- `scripts/features_vocab.py` | the step vocabulary: the generated StepsTyped grammar, a step read back into its
  template, kind and term, the (template, kind) patterns (design §14.1, §14.3)
- `tests/scenarios/vocab.py` | the vocabulary in pytest-bdd: step definitions matched by grammar, the handler registry
  (`from vocab import handler`), the marks from the spec, the U3/U8 checks after a generated scenario
- `tests/scenarios/world_*.py` | shared handlers: the domain kinds' nodes and files (`world_nodes.py`), running a
  command and reading its result (`world_run.py`)

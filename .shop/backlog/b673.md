# spec product, spec actor and spec job keep the product file's other keys and refuse a file of another shape

## Notes

batch-108 review 1 (b602): product_store's read keeps only id, purpose, actors and the jobs' actors, statement and needs, so the next write drops any other key (b642's domain map, b641's serves: would be lost), and a file whose jobs or actors has another type crashes as broken where a refusal naming the file belongs.
Lines product#r8-r13. A product detail is any key or table of the product file product_store's form does not name (the product form: id, purpose, actors, [jobs.<id>] with actors, statement, needs); each declaration keeps it byte for byte (read keeps the parsed data whole, render writes the known keys and then every other key back). A product not of the product form: a file whose jobs, actors or a job's actors/needs/statement are of another type (e.g. jobs = ["j1"], actors = "x"), refused as product not in the product form naming the product file, not broken. How the world must be built so each scenario fails before the fix: product_handlers' Given of a product detail writes the product file with an extra table (e.g. [domain] subdomains = ["s1"]) beside the actor or job the run needs, and the Then of keep reads that table back from the kept file (today render drops it); the Given that this product does not conform to the product form writes jobs = ["j1"] (today read's .items() raises AttributeError: broken, exit not a refusal), and the Thens check an error exit with a refusal (not a traceback), nothing committed, and the output naming .shop/product/product.toml. Technical call: one refusal reason product not in the product form on all three operations.

## Requirements

### product

#### [g8] A product declared again keeps the product's details
- [r8] While this product holds a product detail, when the person declares a product with an id and a purpose, the product service shall keep the product detail in the product and complete the request.

#### [g9] A product actor declared keeps the product's details
- [r9] While this product holds a product detail, when the person declares a product actor, the product service shall keep the product detail in the product and complete the request.

#### [g10] A job declared keeps the product's details
- [r10] While this product holds a product detail, when the person declares a job with an id and a product actor and a job statement, the product service shall keep the product detail in the product and complete the request.

#### [g11] A product not of the product form is not declared again
- [r11] While this product does not conform to the product form, if the person declares a product with an id and a purpose, then the product service shall refuse the request and name this product.

#### [g12] No product actor is declared in a product not of the product form
- [r12] While this product does not conform to the product form, if the person declares a product actor, then the product service shall refuse the request and name this product.

#### [g13] No job is declared in a product not of the product form
- [r13] While this product does not conform to the product form, if the person declares a job with an id and a product actor and a job statement, then the product service shall refuse the request and name this product.

### specification model

```model
object product
  relations: holds → product detail (many)

object product detail kind of text

object product form kind of artifact

service product
  operation declare(product, id, purpose)  outcomes: done → the product | refused: product not in the product form
  operation declare actor(product actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product not in the product form
  operation declare job(job, id, product actor, job statement, need)
    outcomes: done → the job | refused: job statement not in the job statement form, product actor outside the product, need outside the product, need that depends on the job, product not in the product form
```

- **product detail** (noun) → object product detail. A part of the product that this release of the product service declares nothing of, as one a later release wrote, kept as it is by each declaration. Plural: product details.
- **the product form** (proper noun) → object product form. The form a product is kept in: its id and purpose, its actors, and its jobs, each with its actors, its job statement and its needs.

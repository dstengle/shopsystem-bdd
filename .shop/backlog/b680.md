# A job's coordination kind derived from its trace: a port request, a long-running process or a saga

## Notes

Split out of b644 to keep it under the review limit. Decision 0134: spec trace <job> also returns the job's coordination, derived from its trace's lines and the domain model, never declared: a port request (one request of the core through a driven port, its outcomes told apart by the core's statuses, 0121), a long-running process (an object of the core whose lifecycle waits on replies to its requests), a saga (a long-running process with two or more state-changing requests to other contexts, each compensated by an operation, the model's compensated by property, when a later one fails). Lines to write: one a coordination kind, in the product capability on product.trace, the coordination an attribute of a job with Values (noun) of the three kinds; each scenario's world the domain-model fixture with a driven port (checking_handlers_port) and a serving group whose line requests its operation, failing before the fix since spec trace prints no coordination.

## Requirements

### specification model

```model
object job
  attributes: coordination: port request | long-running process | saga
```

- **coordination** (attribute) → attribute job.coordination. How a job's trace coordinates the requests it makes of other bounded contexts, derived from its lines and the domain model, never declared (decision 0134).

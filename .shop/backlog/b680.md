# A job's coordination kind derived from its trace: a port request, a long-running process or a saga

## Notes

Split out of b644 to keep it under the review limit. Decision 0134: spec trace <job> also returns the job's coordination, derived from its trace's lines and the domain model, never declared: a port request (one request of the core through a driven port, its outcomes told apart by the core's statuses, 0121), a long-running process (an object of the core whose lifecycle waits on replies to its requests), a saga (a long-running process with two or more state-changing requests to other contexts, each compensated by an operation, the model's compensated by property, when a later one fails). Lines to write: one a coordination kind, in the product capability on product.trace, the coordination an attribute of a job with Values (noun) of the three kinds; each scenario's world the domain-model fixture with a driven port (checking_handlers_port) and a serving group whose line requests its operation, failing before the fix since spec trace prints no coordination.
Technical calls: the coordination is an attribute of a job (Values (noun): port request, long-running process, saga), derived at spec trace and printed with the trace (text: a 'coordination: <kind>' line; --json: the content's coordination); the most specific kind wins (a saga is also a long-running process). A port request: the trace uses an operation a port line governs and no line handles its outcome; a long-running process: a line handling its outcome uses an operation that performs a lifecycle transition; a saga: two port operations or more, each with a compensated by property. Worlds, each failing before the fix (spec trace prints no coordination): product_handlers_trace's port world (checking_handlers_port's driven port stock, notebook's g10 serving the job, its r10 requesting stock.reserve); for r32 a notebook line on the reservation's refused or done outcome that archives the note (an operation performing a transition of the note's lifecycle); for r33 a second driven-port operation requested by the serving group and both stock operations given 'compensated by <operation>' on their model lines.

## Requirements

### product

#### [g31] A job whose trace makes a port request no line awaits is coordinated by a port request
- [r31] While a requirement group serves a job and a requirement of the requirement group uses an operation and a port line governs the operation and no requirement handles an operation outcome of the operation, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the job whose coordination is `port request`.

#### [g32] A job whose trace awaits a port's reply in a lifecycle is coordinated by a long-running process
- [r32] While a requirement group serves a job and a requirement of the requirement group uses an operation and a port line governs the operation and a requirement that handles an operation outcome of the operation uses an operation that performs a transition, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the job whose coordination is `long-running process`.

#### [g33] A job whose trace makes compensated requests of two ports is coordinated by a saga
- [r33] While a requirement group serves a job and a requirement of the requirement group uses two operations in a driven port that have a property that refers to an operation, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the job whose coordination is `saga`.

### specification model

```model
object job
  attributes: coordination: port request | long-running process | saga
```

- **coordination** (attribute) → attribute job.coordination. How a job's trace coordinates the requests it makes of other bounded contexts, derived from its lines and the domain model, never declared (decision 0134).
- **port request** (noun) → value job.coordination port request. A job's coordination that is one request of the core through a driven port, its outcomes told apart by the core's statuses.
- **long-running process** (noun) → value job.coordination long-running process. A job's coordination that is an object of the core whose lifecycle waits on the replies to its requests.
- **saga** (noun) → value job.coordination saga. A job's coordination that is a long-running process with two state-changing requests or more to other bounded contexts, each compensated by an operation when a later one fails.

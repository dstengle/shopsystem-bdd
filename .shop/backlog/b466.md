#### [g89] A line removed from an in-batch item leaves its task's workspace too, not only the batch's
- [r89] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and a task of the item is `running` and the workspace of the batch holds a requirement of the item and the workspace of the task holds the requirement and the item does not hold the requirement, when the person amends the item, the batches service shall keep a workspace of the batch that does not hold the requirement and keep a workspace of the task that does not hold the requirement.

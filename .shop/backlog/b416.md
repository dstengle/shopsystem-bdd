# A response's thing that a trigger's thing has ('return the items of the checklist') is brought in by a Given before the When

## Notes

UX trial 2 (2026-11-20, 0.40.0): 'When the person lists a checklist, the checklists service shall return the items of the checklist.' generated 'Given a checklist c1 / When the person lists the checklist c1 / Then the checklists service returns the item i1 / And the checklist c1 has the item i1': i1 is brought in by no Given, and the row that presupposes it is a Then. spec_steps4_boxes.bring_response brings in only the things of an in row whose K1 no act names (b286); an of row to a trigger's thing on an act that makes nothing (return) is the same case. The task agent's binding made the item itself.

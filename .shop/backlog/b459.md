# work split and work retitle

## Notes

From the missing-commands and new-user-flow research of 2026-11-21: Part of 22 hand commits that merge, split or retitle items. Merging folds into b281: add "with its reason and the item it went into" there rather than filing it. Split copies the selected lines to a new item with provenance; retitle keeps the id. The person's words in quote-68.

## Requirements

### items

#### [g31] An open item retitled keeps its id and takes the new title
- [r31] While the status of an item is one of `idea`, `shaped`, `ready`, `in-batch` or `needs-input`, when the person retitles the item with a title, the backlog service shall keep the item and the title of the item.

#### [g32] A closed item is not retitled
- [r32] While the status of an item is one of `delivered`, `refused` or `withdrawn`, if the person retitles the item, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and name the item.

### tracking model

```model
object item
  relations: derives from → item (one)

service backlog
  operation retitle(item, title)  outcomes: done | refused: item not open
  operation split(item, requirement)  outcomes: done → the item | refused: item not an idea or shaped, requirement not in the item
```

- **retitle** (verb, action) → operation backlog.retitle. Forms: retitles, retitled. Frame: <actor> retitles <item>.
- **split** (verb, action) → operation backlog.split. Forms: splits, split. Frame: <actor> splits <item>.
- **derive** (verb, state) → relation item.derives from. An item split from another names that item as the one its lines came from. Forms: derives, derived. Frame: <item> derives from <item>.

# Publish, fetch and obtain request a contract registry port, with a shop-folder adapter

## Notes

quote-94, decision 0139: the specification's contract commands request a driven port (keep a contract at a version, find the contract of a bounded context at a version); the folder layouts of b348/b587/b591/b593 (.shop/contracts/<version>/, provider/<id>/<version>/ in an adapter spec) become the first adapter, wired by a technical decision; obtaining from a context of the same shop goes through it (was b622). Delivering it supersedes decisions 0137 and 0139 at requirement tier.

## Requirements

### contract-registry

narrator: the specification service
group: service
purpose: The contracts the specification keeps and finds through a driven port: a contract deposited at a version, and the contract of a bounded context retrieved at a version, wherever an adapter keeps them.
area: registry

#### [g1] A contract deposited is kept by the registry
- [r1] When the specification service deposits a contract, the contract registry service shall complete the request.

#### [g2] A contract retrieved is returned by the registry
- [r2] When the specification service retrieves a contract, the contract registry service shall return the contract.

### checking

#### [g124] A contract published is deposited in the contract registry at its version
- [r124] When the person publishes a contract with a version, the specification service shall deposit the contract with the version.

### registry model

```model
service contract registry
  operation deposit(contract, version)  outcomes: done → the contract | refused: version already deposited
  operation retrieve(contract, bounded context, version)  outcomes: returned → the contract | refused: contract not deposited
```

- **the contract registry service** (proper noun) → service contract registry. Where this bounded context's contracts are kept and found, whichever adapter keeps them (decision 0139).
- **deposit** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.deposit. The specification service gives the contract registry a contract to keep at its version. Forms: deposits, deposited. Frame: <actor> deposits <contract>.
- **retrieve** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.retrieve. The specification service asks the contract registry for the contract of a bounded context at a version. Forms: retrieves, retrieved. Frame: <actor> retrieves <contract>.

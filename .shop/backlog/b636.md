## Requirements

### checking

#### [g127] A contract published is deposited in the contract registry at its version
- [r127] When the person publishes a contract with a version, the specification service shall deposit the contract with the version.

#### [g125] A contract fetched is retrieved from the contract registry at its version
- [r125] When a downstream context fetches a contract with a version, the specification service shall retrieve the contract with the version.

#### [g126] A contract obtained is retrieved from the contract registry for its bounded context and version
- [r126] When the person obtains a contract from a bounded context with a version, the specification service shall retrieve the contract with the bounded context and the version.

#### [g128] A deposit the contract registry completes publishes the contract
- [r128] When the contract registry service completes the request to deposit a contract, the specification service shall set the status of the contract to `published`.

#### [g129] A contract the contract registry returns is returned to whoever asked for it
- [r129] When the contract registry service returns the request to retrieve a contract, the specification service shall return the contract.

#### [g130] A retrieval the contract registry refuses names the version and changes nothing
- [r130] When the contract registry service refuses the request to retrieve a contract, the specification service shall name the version of the contract and change nothing.

### contract-registry

narrator: the specification service
group: service
purpose: The contracts the specification keeps and finds through a driven port: a contract deposited at a version, and the contract of a bounded context retrieved at a version, wherever an adapter keeps them.
area: registry
kind: port

#### [g1] A contract deposited is kept by the registry
- [r1] When the specification service deposits a contract, the contract registry service shall complete the request.
Relied on by: checking#r127

#### [g2] A contract retrieved is returned by the registry
- [r2] When the specification service retrieves a contract, the contract registry service shall return the contract.
Relied on by: checking#r125

#### [g3] A contract retrieved at a version never deposited is refused by the registry
- [r3] If the specification service retrieves a contract with a version that no contract has, then the contract registry service shall refuse the request and name the version.
Relied on by: checking#r126

### registry model

```model
service contract registry
  operation deposit(contract, version)  outcomes: done → the contract
  operation retrieve(contract, bounded context, version)  outcomes: returned → the contract | refused: version not deposited
```

- **the contract registry service** (proper noun) → service contract registry. Where this bounded context's contracts are kept and found, whichever adapter keeps them (decision 0139).
- **deposit** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.deposit. The specification service gives the contract registry a contract to keep at its version. Forms: deposits, deposited. Frame: <actor> deposits <contract>.
- **retrieve** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.retrieve. The specification service asks the contract registry for the contract of a bounded context at a version. Forms: retrieves, retrieved. Frame: <actor> retrieves <contract>.

## Answers

- Q: Review f1 (fix now): the new driven port's three outcomes (contract registry.deposit done, .retrieve returned, .retrieve refused: version not deposited) reach no core line and no port line excludes them, so spec check on main would report three v_pb2 errors (decision 0127). They cannot be handled as the model stands: v_pb2 joins a core line's trigger on the port's outcome with the operation's object (op_object), and the request pack's complete and refuse take only a request, while deposit's and retrieve's object is contract (not a kind of request, unlike the rule's exemplar reservation); only 'When the contract registry service returns a contract' can be said, covering retrieve returned alone. Which way: (a) reshape the registry model so each operation's outcome object is a kind of request (e.g. object deposit kind of request, object retrieval kind of request; deposit done -> the deposit; retrieve returned -> the retrieval) and add checking core lines on each outcome (on completion of a deposit or a retrieval, and on the refusal of a retrieval naming the version), changing r1-r3's reading; (b) add port lines that exclude outcomes (only refused/failed make sense to exclude, so done and returned still need core lines); or (c) treat it as a gap of v_pb2/the request pack (an outcome on a non-request object can never be handled) and fix the rule first in its own item, re-laying b636 after. The implementation on branch batch-125-b636 (commits b1db1fb84, 2d3e96b01, 2ec4a8e1d; contract_registry, its shop-folder adapter, the stand-in registry, test_contract_registry) passes the whole suite and can be kept.
  A: (c): the gap is the language's, not the registry model's: a core line cannot be triggered on a port's reply about the request's object (b626, a reply trigger names the request's object), so v_pb2 cannot be met. b636 waits on b626 (and v_pb2 matching an outcome through it); its work is kept on branch batch-125-b636 (b1db1fb84, 2d3e96b01, 2ec4a8e1d) to cherry-pick; then add core lines handling deposit done, retrieve returned and retrieve refused.

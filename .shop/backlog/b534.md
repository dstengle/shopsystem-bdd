# A term proposal is told from a requirement line by the step that kept it, not by its prefix

## Notes

batch-74 review 1 observations f3, f4: request_terms.is_proposal reads a line opening with '- **' as a term proposal, so such a line given to work propose skips grammar 4; and request_terms.entries splits a definition that quotes another bullet ' - **' into two entries.
batch-74 review 2 observation: with no domain model, request_terms.term_entry drops the element, so a proposal with no definition or a verb/attribute entry (needing Frame:/Values:) is accepted into an unreadable term; refuse the accept naming the proposal.

# A response's thing owned by an indexical ('the items of this backlog') or a While part's thing is brought in by a Given before the When, tested

## Notes

batch-52 review 1 (PR #76): spec_steps4_bring's owner takes a row K1 | of | K2 only when K2's origin is while or trigger, so a This: referent (origin indexical) leaves its K1 mentioned by a Then no Given introduces, which scenarios#r33 forbids; and feature_file_tools_collections' owned lines have no While part, so the While owner case is unexercised (e.g. 'While a stage is open, when the person runs `work status`, `work` shall print the lines of the stage').
Shaping (2026-11-20): two lines of scenarios beside r33 (b416), one group each so that each scenario's Givens pick its own example: a requirement that uses of-relation and this ('return the items of this backlog', a This: referent of origin indexical, which spec_steps4_bring.owner does not take today) and one that uses of-relation and while ('While a stage is open, when the person runs `work status`, `work` shall print the lines of the stage', taken but unexercised). Build: owner takes an indexical K2 too; feature_file_tools_collections (or feature_file_tools_uses) gives each case its example by the Given facts 'requirement use `this`' and 'requirement use `while`'. Rated: medium value (scenarios#r33's guarantee holds for every owner), low urgency, small size.

# model logic stays near-linear as a spec grows to thousands of lines

## Notes

Measured 2026-11-09 (scaled copies of this repository's 265 lines, each copy its own commands): grounding grammar/check.lp takes 0.05 s at 265 lines, 0.83 s at 1,045, 6.7 s at 2,605, 27.7 s at 5,205 (about 550x the time for 20x the lines); solving stays under 0.4 s and the candidate pairs grow linearly (about 1.3 a line). Nearly all the grounding is the candidate-pair index (check.lp lines 29-68): cc/can compare responses of every pair of lines before the trigger filter (1.3M pairs considered for 3,332 kept at 2,605 lines), and kin/doerk carry the rest. Filtering by shared trigger first keeps every finding and cuts about 40%, still quadratic.

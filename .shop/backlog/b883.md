# An item given back at opening frees its share of the review limit for the items it pushed out

## Notes

Seen at batch-175's first opening: b873 was picked first, b877 left out as over the review limit after b873's estimate, then b873 was given back (its scenarios passed on the lay) and the pick laid again without it, leaving nothing: 'no batch started'. When an item is given back, the items the limit left out because of it are picked again, so the batch opens with what fits.

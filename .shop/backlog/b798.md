# The product commands' refusals name their cause and their option, and check the actors they are given

## Notes

New-user trial of 2026-11-26 (quote-103): spec job --statement 'When x, the cat wants y, so they can z.' refused as not of the job statement form while the cause is the unknown actor; a statement naming another actor than --actor accepted; spec plays 'the cat' refused as "product_actor 'the cat'" where siblings say --actor; spec plays --as accepts any actor name without checking the model.
From the new user's trial in quote-103 and the person's quote-104: spec job refused 'When x, the cat wants y, so they can z.' as not of the job statement form, its cause a one-character situation the form did not take, with an unknown actor in it besides; a statement mentioning another actor than --actor was kept; spec plays spelled its refusal 'product_actor' where its siblings say the option (the CLI adapter's spelling: the positional's metavar should be 'product actor', checked by the r25 scenario's named text); spec plays --as took an actor the domain model does not declare.
Rated medium value (a new user's first product declarations refused or kept wrongly, each a minor finding of the trial), medium urgency (the wave of the trial's fixes), small size (four refusals and one form fix in product_layer and product_assign, the plays positional's metavar).

## Requirements

### product

#### [g37] A job whose statement mentions an actor the product does not hold is refused
- [r37] If the person declares a job with a job statement that mentions a product actor that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the product actor.

#### [g38] A job whose statement mentions another actor than the job's is refused
- [r38] If the person declares a job with a product actor and a job statement that mentions a different product actor, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job statement.

#### [g39] A job whose statement's situation is one word is declared
- [r39] When the person declares a job with a job statement that conforms to the job statement form, the product service shall keep the job in the product and complete the request.

```given
the job statement `j2` holds "When x, the developer wants y, so they can z."
```

#### [g40] A product actor is not mapped to an actor the domain model does not hold
- [r40] If the person maps a product actor with this bounded context and an actor that the domain model does not hold, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the actor.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation declare job(job, id, product actor, job statement, need)
    outcomes: done → the job | refused: job statement not in the job statement form, product actor outside the product, need outside the product, need that depends on the job, product not in the product form, job statement mentioning another product actor
  operation map(product actor, bounded context, actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product actor outside the product, actor outside the domain model
```

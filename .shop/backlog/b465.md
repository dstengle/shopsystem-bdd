# work withdraw takes its reason

## Requirements

### items

retires: [r41 r18]

#### [g41] An open item withdrawn with its reason becomes withdrawn
- [r41] While the status of an item is one of `idea`, `shaped`, `ready` or `needs-input`, when the person withdraws the item with a reason, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `withdrawn` and keep the item.

### tracking model

```model

  operation withdraw(item, reason)  outcomes: done
```

# work merge and work annotate refusals stated: a successor that is the item or closed, an in-batch item

## Requirements

### items

#### [g40] An in-batch item is not merged
- [r40] While the status of an item is `in-batch`, if the person merges the item, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and name the item.

#### [g41] An in-batch item takes no note
- [r41] While the status of an item is `in-batch`, if the person annotates the item, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and name the item.

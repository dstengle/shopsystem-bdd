# Read what parses: only and a response's count over a relation translated for model logic and features generate, and model logic naming each line it does not read

## Notes

From .shop/records/2026-11-15-collections-review.md (claims 4, 5 and the silent skip): 'a batch that holds only items ...' and 'a batch that holds at most three items' parse but spec_lf_facts refuses them ('no translation of only over that', 'no translation of a count over that'), and model logic then prints no findings and exits 0 because ctx.refused (scripts/spec_context.py:100) is read only by features generate. The person's words: quote-29, quote-31.
What it builds. (1) spec_lf_facts (with spec_lf_quant) reads an only box and a count box whose scope is a relation row or a state frame on a response's object ("print a batch that holds only items whose status is `ready`", "holds at most three items", "holds exactly three items"; R5, R7) as restrictions of that object, beside oattr and orel: check.lp keeps them as facts and proves nothing of them (decision 0093); they oblige no act of their own, agree with any line on the act, and clash with nothing new. A count over a relation in a response that admits no K obliges no hold. (2) spec_steps4_boxes writes the Then of each as the property, said of the printed thing ("the batch b1 holds only items whose status is `ready`", "the batch b1 holds at most three items"), read back by features_vocab4 and the step grammar 4 (spec_rgl_steps); its handler checks it on the output. The Givens give the data the trigger needs; a counter-example (a non-member, one past the bound) is b273's. (3) model logic (spec_solve, its text and --json reports) prints each requirement of the capabilities it checks that ctx.refused holds (spec_context.py:100), by its id with what it has no translation of (spec_lf_facts' refusal text), and never prints `no findings` while one is unread. Decision: the exit stays the findings' (an unread line alone exits 0, as r261 says of a codified-2 capability), because the time constructs of language.md's known limit #16 are unread today and the readiness bar runs model logic, so an error exit would refuse every item holding a time line; the bar may later choose to refuse on it. No construct code is new: only, count and holds-relation are grammar/constructs.md's; language.md's "What parses is read" gains the two shapes, each with a reading block. Leaves out: an order, membership said from the member's side, an empty collection (b267, b274), counter-examples (b273). The person's words: quote-29, quote-31 (quote-30 for the empty collection, b274's).

Rated 2026-11-15: high value, because a `no findings` that can mean "not checked" undermines every `model logic` result; high urgency, because b273, b274 and b267 depend on it; medium size, because it is two translations in spec_lf_facts and spec_lf_quant, their steps in spec_steps4_boxes, and `model logic`'s report of the requirements it has no translation of.

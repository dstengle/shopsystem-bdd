# The upstream settles every line in one turn, a counter-proposal included

## Notes

From the black-box test of b26 on real GitHub (.shop/records/2026-11-05-b26-github-blackbox.md): `work outcome rewritten` passes the turn at once (intake r7), so the upstream cannot ask about or settle the other lines in the same turn; it must leave its rewrite for last. A counter-proposal could wait for `work turn` like every other outcome.

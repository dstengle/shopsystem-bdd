# An order over a member kind's own sense of a word: order-by and order-by-every take the attribute entry of the member kind, not the first entry of its word

## Notes

Fixes batch-35 defect-2 (.shop/batches/35/defect-2.md) of b275: spec_lf_ordered.member takes the first attribute entry whose term is the order's word, so 'print every upgrade step, ordered by release, in ascending order' finds release of repository and features generate crashes. The entry is the member kind's own (up its kind tree). The person's words: quote-29. Same root as b287 (defect-1: senses of one word).
What it builds: spec_lf_ordered.member finds the order's attribute among the entries of its word by the member kind (up its kind tree), as spec_lf_ids finds an attribute up grammar 4's kind tree; with no entry of the member kind the order is refused naming the term. Its lines say the case through the generated scenario: a term of two senses and a requirement that uses it with order-by or order-by-every gets its scenario with the order counter-example (today features generate crashes, defect-2). grammar/constructs.md's order-by-every example (or a second one) shows a word of two senses so model constructs --check proves it. Depends on nothing; same root as b287 (value-compare over two senses).

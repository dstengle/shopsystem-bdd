# Verification routes on design-decisions

## Notes

Design record .shop/records/2026-11-09-cnl-4/design.md, section 10.3 I3 (and section 4.1): a decision may carry `verified_by: [scenario | check | judgement | monitor]` beside enforced_by; `work decision --verified-by <route>` writes it; `model check` refuses a route outside the four (v_d9) and warns of a universal condition that no decision's verified_by covers (w_unrouted; a decision covers a universal condition by citing its id in rests_on or in its text, the builder's choice). This repository's U4, U5, U7, U8, U11 get their routes in this item (check after each scenario for U4, U8, U11; judgement for U5, U7). Its part of b112 (the person's words are in b112's notes): each requirement is a premise with its own verification route, and only ubiquitous lines need one, carried by the decision, never inside the sentence.

Rated 2026-11-10: medium value, because it makes the verification of a ubiquitous line explicit on its decision, which the 4.0 language rests on but nothing breaks without; medium urgency, because only the decision-tiers reword (I27) depends on it, though it can go in the first batch (I0-I5); small, because it is one field read, written and checked, plus five routes on this repository's decisions (one batch task).

## Requirements

### decision-tiers

- **unrouted** (adjective of universal condition): a universal condition that no design-decision's `verified_by` covers.
- **misrouted** (adjective of design-decision): a design-decision whose `verified_by` names a route other than `scenario`, `check`, `judgement` or `monitor`.

#### [g10] `work decision --verified-by` writes a route
- [r10] Where `--verified-by` is given, when the person runs `work decision`, `work` shall write a design-decision and commit the design-decision.

#### [g11] `model check` names a universal condition with no route
- [r11] When the person runs `model check` on a universal condition that is unrouted, `model` shall print the universal condition.

#### [g12] `model check` refuses a route outside the four
- [r12] If the person runs `model check` on a design-decision that is misrouted, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the design-decision.

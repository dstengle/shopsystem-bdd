# A response's 'the K' binds the trigger's new K, not the While part's

## Notes

Found by the contracts examples: in 'While a charge for an order exists, when the ordering service secures a charge for the order, the charges service shall return the charge', 'the charge' binds the trigger's charge (return(e2, ..., object: x4)), not the While part's.

# spec assign refuses a job the product does not declare

## Notes

New-user trial 2 of 2026-11-26 (quote-104): spec assign find-books --to bounded-context/example.org/bookclub/club printed 'assigned the job find-books ...' exit 0 for a job never declared; spec check then warned w_unserved_job of it as a real job.
New-user trial 2 (the person's words in quote-104): spec assign of a job the product never declared assigned it, exit 0, and spec check then warned w_unserved_job of it. The product service's assign is to refuse a job that is not in the product, naming the job, as declare subdomain does (product#r17).
Rated high value and urgency: a MAJOR trial finding, an undeclared job assigned silently; small: one refusal reason and its check.

## Requirements

### product

#### [g59] A job the product does not hold is not assigned
- [r59] If the person assigns a job that is not in the product to a bounded context, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation assign(job, bounded context)  outcomes: done → the job | refused: job assigned already, id not in the context id form, bounded context outside the product, job outside the product
```

# Posting a step does not depend on reading the issue first, nor take the other side's comment as its own

## Notes

From batch-9 review 4 (observation on b36's f6 fix): scripts/request_post.py committed now calls unrecorded (gh issue view) before every comment, so a failed view refuses a step that could have been posted; and any comment the record does not hold whose stripped body matches is taken as this step's earlier post, including an unsynced comment from the other side (today the sides' bodies differ, so no client hits it). Consider tolerating a failed view and matching only this side's own posts.

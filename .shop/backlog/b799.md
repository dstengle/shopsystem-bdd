# work line --serves refuses a job the product does not hold and confirms the line it added

## Notes

New-user trial of 2026-11-26 (quote-103): work line b2 --into rota '<line>' --serves no-such-job printed 'rota#g1 serves no-such-job', exit 0; with a known job it prints only that pair, not the line added.
New-user trial of 2026-11-26, the person's words in quote-103 and quote-104: work line --serves takes a job the product does not hold and prints only the pair, not the line it added.
Rated: value 3, a job no product holds was taken silently in the trial and the line added went unconfirmed; urgency 3, part of the new-user wave; size 2, a refusal and a printed line in backlog_serves. Build: the scenario of the confirmation runs work line --into drafting '<line>' --serves <job> and reads the line and the pair printed.

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g92] Dedicating a requirement group to a job the product does not hold is refused
- [r92] While the product exists, if the person dedicates a requirement group to a job that the product does not hold with an item, then the drafting service shall refuse the request and name the job.

#### [g93] A requirement drafted for a job is named with the job
- [r93] When the person dedicates a requirement group to a job with an item and a requirement, the drafting service shall keep the requirement in the item and name the requirement, the requirement group and the job.

# An item whose only lines are moves passes the readiness bar

## Notes

Defect of b607 (batch-99 defect-1): readiness_bar.well_formed refuses an item of moves only ('it has no requirement line') since it does not count the moves block beside universal conditions, retirements and adapter blocks; b617 and b608 are moves alone and wait on it. Fix: count blocks.moves there.

## Requirements

### items

#### [g64] A shaped item whose only lines are model lines that move is readied
- [r64] While an item is `shaped` and the item holds a model line that moves to an area and the item holds no requirement and the item meets the readiness bar, when the person readies the item, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `ready`.

# An item makes a new area with its kind and the areas it imports

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g85] A capability drafted with a driven port keeps the port in the item
- [r85] When the person drafts an item with a capability and a driven port, the drafting service shall keep the capability in the item and keep the driven port that holds the capability in the item.

### batches

#### [g149] A new capability laid with its driven port is kept in an area of kind port
- [r149] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item holds a capability and the item holds a driven port that holds the capability, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall keep the driven port that holds the capability.

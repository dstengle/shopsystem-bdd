# An item makes a new capability's area a driven port, importing the areas its lines read

## Notes

Split out of b636's shaping: b636's contract registry is a driven port (an area of kind port, decision 0139), but an item's new capability's area is made by the lay with kind: core and imports: [] (backlog_model_area's skeleton), so no item can make a port that reads the specification's words (v_m1). Lines: drafting r85 (the area a driven port, given as the draft's area parameter of the subkind driven port) and batches r149 (the lay keeps it as an area of kind port). Technical calls: work line --capability <c> --area <a> --port (a cli-only flag of drafting.draft, the block's new field kind: port); the lay's skeleton writes the block's kind, and imports: each area that declares an element the item's model lines of the area name or whose layer has a term the capability's lines use (so the made area raises no v_m1). Worlds that fail first: r85 work line --capability with --port on an item: today the option is unknown (usage error); r149 a ready item meeting the bar with a new capability in a new driven port whose model line names an object of the specification area: today the batch lays spec/model/<area>.md with kind: core.
Rated: high value (no item can make a driven port today, so no port-backed item, b636 first, can be laid), high urgency (b636 waits on it), small size (backlog_capability's option and field, the skeleton's kind and imports, two scenarios).
Technical call added in shaping b636: the lay also adds a driven port the item makes to the imports: of each area whose capability lines on the item use the port's words (b636's checking lines read registry's deposit and retrieve from the specification area), and takes it out again on the unlay; without it b636's lines raise v_m1.

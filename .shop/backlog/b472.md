# A command writes a term pack (spec/packs/<name>.md) and its entries

## Notes

spec forms lists the term pack (spec/packs/<name>.md) as written by hand. A command writes a term into a context's own pack on an item (work line <b> --pack <name> --term '<bullet>'), kept on the item as vocabulary entries are, and work batch start lays it: spec/packs/<name>.md made when missing (## Glossary), the entry appended, the pack's name added to spec/index.md's imports:. A term the pack has already is refused naming the term. Implementation also refuses a pack name of the plugin's own packs (grammar/packs/), since a context pack of that name shadows the plugin's whole pack (spec_packs.path_of). Once delivered, spec forms names the command for the term pack. The person's words in quote-68.

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g28] A term written into a pack for an item is kept
- [r28] When the person writes a term into a pack with an item, the drafting service shall keep the term in the item.

#### [g29] A term that its pack has already is refused
- [r29] While a pack has a term, if the person writes the term into the pack with an item, then the drafting service shall refuse the request and name the term.

### batches

#### [g78] A batch opened keeps each term of a pack in the pack, and the pack imported
- [r78] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item holds a term of a pack, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall keep the pack that has the term and keep this bounded context that imports the pack.

### tracking model

```model
service drafting
  operation write into pack(term, pack, item)  outcomes: done | refused: term in the pack
```

- **write** (verb, action) → operation drafting.write into pack. Forms: writes, wrote, written. Frame: <actor> writes <term> into <pack>.

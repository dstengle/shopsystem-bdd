# features generate writes a response's thing's restrictions after the act that brings it, not before

## Notes

Also seen there: r11 'While a viewer subscribes to no channel of a video' given as 'the viewer v1 subscribes to no channel', more than the line says; that is language.md's documented 'Given of a no box beside the link it is said by' limit, so no item of its own.
Fixes first (quote-29), beside quote-30 and quote-31's collections. The defect is live in delivered features: spec_steps4.Reader.acts writes a response's items in body order, and spec_lf puts a response object's restrictions (an attribute value, a relation row, a relative clause, the scope of an every box in its relative clause) before the event that brings the object in, so features/backlog.feature's g22, g32, g33 and g35 say 'Then the status of the item i1 is `idea`' before 'And `work` writes the item i1', and the same order shows in backlog g5, readiness's `work line --relation` and the others, requests' `work request` and telemetry's measurement scenarios; reproduced on a copy of spec/ with one line each of an attribute ('write an item whose status is `idea`'), a relation ('write a dependency on the item'), a relative clause ('write a batch that holds an item') and every ('a batch that holds every item whose status is `ready`'); only and a count over a relation are refused today (b272), and every, only and a count over a printed object put their restrictor in the Givens, which is right. The collections review's 'did not show up' was wrong. Build: spec_steps4's acts (and spec_steps4_boxes' acts where it walks a scope) hold back each response atom that mentions a response referent no earlier act has brought in, and write it after the first act whose event names that referent; atoms of referents brought in before (While, trigger, an earlier act) keep their place; an image (its T) stays where it is. Every regenerated feature changes only in its Thens' order; features generate --check and the scenario tests run as before. r111's own scenario is the regression: its response 'a description of the referent that follows the act' is itself a response object with a relation, so its generated Then must say '`features` writes the description d1' first. New terms referent, bring in, description, precede, follow and the rows act of requirement, description of referent are feature-file-tools'. Leaves out the Givens of a no box beside its link (language.md's limit) and b272's only and counts over a relation. No dependencies.

Rated 2026-11-15: medium value, because every generated scenario with a response object reads in order once it is fixed, though no test passes or fails differently; medium urgency, because b267's ordered Thens need it and the person asked for the fixes first (quote-29); small, because it is one ordering pass in spec_steps4's acts (and spec_steps4_boxes' walk of a scope), the regenerated features changing only in the order of their Thens.

### feature-file-tools

- **referent** (noun): a thing that a noun phrase of a requirement names, which a step of its scenario names by an instance name such as `i1`. Kind of: thing.
- **bring in** (verb, state): an act of a requirement's response names a referent that no part of the requirement before the act names. Forms: brings in, brought in. Frame: act brings in referent.
- **description** (noun): a step that says a value of an attribute of a referent, a relation of the referent or a relative clause on the referent, which the requirement of its scenario says beside the act that brings in the referent, written as a Then line of the scenario. Kind of: text.
- **precede** (verb, state): a line of a scenario comes before the step that says an act in the scenario. Forms: precedes, preceded. Frame: text precedes act.
- **follow** (verb, state): a line of a scenario comes after the step that says an act in the scenario. Forms: follows, followed. Frame: text follows act.

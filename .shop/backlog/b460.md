# Lines amended on an in-batch item

## Notes

From the missing-commands and new-user-flow research of 2026-11-21: b383 stops "before it is in a batch" and b431 covers ready items only. 38–59 hand commits changed lines of ready or in-batch items. `work amend <item>` relays the item on `batch-<n>` and its task branch. The person's words in quote-68.

## Requirements

### batches

#### [g77] An in-batch item amended has its lines laid again on its batch's and its task's workspaces
- [r77] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and the item holds a requirement, when the person amends the item, the batches service shall keep a workspace of the batch that holds the requirement and keep a workspace of a task of the item that holds the requirement.

#### [g78] An in-batch item amended has a line it no longer holds taken out of the batch's workspace
- [r78] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and the workspace of the batch holds a requirement of the item and the item does not hold the requirement, when the person amends the item, the batches service shall keep a workspace of the batch that does not hold the requirement.

### execution model

```model
service batches
  operation amend(item)  outcomes: done | refused: item not in batch
```

- **amend** (verb, action) → operation batches.amend. An item's lines laid again on the workspaces of its batch and of its task after they changed. Forms: amends, amended. Frame: <actor> amends <item>.

# Entailment follows a request from one line to another's trigger

## Notes

L-3 of the contracts design, first slice, after the two items above. entail.lp gains a stratum: a premise's act requesting an operation fires the lines triggered by that request of the same requester, and their outcomes fire the lines triggered on them; depth bounded, the chain of premises kept for the findings. Today a premise is only a line on the query's own trigger, so no provider line is ever one (decision 0118). Measure the grounding cost on the adapter examples.

# A job's trace derived from its serving groups, the requests they reach and the jobs it needs

## Notes

Decisions 0140, 0142. A job's trace, derived and never listed: the lines of its serving groups, every line reached through the requests they make (the entailment chain's covers), to a fixed depth, and the needed jobs' traces joined; reported by an operation of the product service, CLI spec trace <job>. Needs b641 (serves:) and b602 (jobs and needs).
Rated: medium value (a report that the product check's actor and participant rules read), low urgency, medium size (about three lines; reuses the entailment chain's covers).
Technical calls: the trace is the operation product.trace, CLI spec trace <job> (text by default, the envelope with --json), returning the requirements by id with their text and the adapter specs by id, each with why it is in the trace (served by g<n>, governs the operation r<n> uses, realises port line r<n>, needed job j<n>); a reached line's own requests followed again up to the entailment chain's DEPTH (spec_entail_chain), each requirement once. Provider operations an adapter spec uses (decision 0138) are b637's, not built: the trace stops at the adapter spec. The coordination kind (decision 0134) is split out to the item that depends on this one. Scenario worlds, each failing before the fix (no spec trace command exists): the domain-model fixture with a product file holding jobs j1 and j2 (j1 needs j2); r15 a notebook group serving j1 whose line is returned; r16 that line requesting notes.archive and another capability's line governing archive, returned; r17 the driven port stock fixture of checking_handlers_port with a port line governing stock.reserve, a core line of the serving group requesting it, and an adapter spec under it realising the port line's id, the adapter spec returned; r18 j1 served by nothing but its need j2 served by a group, j2's line returned when tracing j1; r19 a job j3 no group serves, refused naming j3.

## Requirements

### product

#### [g15] A job's trace holds the requirements of the groups that serve it
- [r15] While a requirement group serves a job and the requirement group has a requirement, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the requirement.

#### [g16] A job's trace holds the requirements that govern the operations its requirements use
- [r16] While a requirement group serves a job and a requirement of the requirement group uses an operation and a different requirement governs the operation, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the requirement.

#### [g17] A job's trace holds the adapter specs that realise the port lines it reaches
- [r17] While a requirement group serves a job and a requirement of the requirement group uses an operation and a port line governs the operation and an adapter spec realises the requirement id of the port line, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the adapter spec.

#### [g18] A job's trace holds the traces of the jobs it needs
- [r18] While a requirement group serves a need and the requirement group has a requirement, when the person traces a job that needs the need, the product service shall return the requirement.

#### [g19] A job that no requirement group serves is not traced
- [r19] If the person traces a job that no requirement group serves, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation trace(job)  outcomes: returned | refused: job that no requirement group serves
```

- **trace** (verb, action) → operation product.trace. The person asks which requirements and adapter specs a job of the product rests on: the lines of the requirement groups that serve it, the lines its requests reach and the traces of the jobs it needs. Forms: traces, traced. Frame: <actor> traces <job>.

### cli adapter

```toml

command = "spec trace"
[operations."product.trace".parameters]
job = { positional = 1, example = "j1" }
```

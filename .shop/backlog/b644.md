# A job's trace derived from its serving groups, the requests they reach and the jobs it needs

## Notes

Decisions 0140, 0142. A job's trace, derived and never listed: the lines of its serving groups, every line reached through the requests they make (the entailment chain's covers), to a fixed depth, and the needed jobs' traces joined; reported by an operation of the product service, CLI spec trace <job>. Needs b641 (serves:) and b602 (jobs and needs).
Rated: medium value (a report that the product check's actor and participant rules read), low urgency, medium size (about three lines; reuses the entailment chain's covers).

## Requirements

### product

#### [g15] A job's trace holds the requirements of the groups that serve it
- [r15] While a requirement group serves a job and the requirement group has a requirement, when the person traces the job, the product service shall return the requirement.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation trace(job)  outcomes: returned | refused: job that no requirement group serves
```

- **trace** (verb, action) → operation product.trace. The person asks which requirements and adapter specs a job of the product rests on: the lines of the requirement groups that serve it, the lines its requests reach and the traces of the jobs it needs. Forms: traces, traced. Frame: <actor> traces <job>.

### cli adapter

```toml

command = "spec trace"
[operations."product.trace".parameters]
job = { positional = 1, example = "j1" }
```

# The product check over the jobs, the domain map and the capabilities

## Notes

Decisions 0140-0144. The product check, run by spec check in a shop that holds the product layer: each job assigned to exactly one bounded context and served by exactly one requirement group or more of one capability in it (0140); each of a job's actors, under its context's term, the subject of a trigger of a line of its serving groups or its trace (0141); a subdomain no bounded context addresses; each capability an entry point, a participant, a port, or an orphan reported (0144); a job's statement in its form. Needs b641 (serves:), b642 (the domain map) and b644 (the trace, for actors and participants).
Rated: high value (the checks are what make the product layer more than notes), low urgency (needs b641, b642, b644 first), medium size (about six lines, each a rule of spec check).

## Requirements

### checking

#### [g113] A job served here that the product assigns to another bounded context fails the check
- [r113] While a requirement group serves a job and this bounded context has the job and another bounded context holds the job, if the person validates this specification, then the specification service shall fail the check and name the job.

### specification model

```model
object job
  relations: reaches → requirement | capability | bounded context (many)

object actor
  relations: triggers → requirement (many); plays → product actor (many)
```

- **reach** (verb, state) → relation job.reaches. A requirement, a capability or a bounded context is in a job's trace: the job's serving groups, the lines their requests reach and the traces of the jobs it needs hold it or a line of it. Forms: reaches, reached. Frame: <job> reaches <requirement>. Frame: <job> reaches <capability>. Frame: <job> reaches <bounded context>.
- **trigger** (verb, state) → relation actor.triggers. An actor is the subject of the trigger of a requirement, as the person is of "When the person validates this specification". Forms: triggers, triggered. Frame: <actor> triggers <requirement>.
- **address** (verb, state) → relation bounded context.addresses. A bounded context of the domain map gives the subdomain its solution. Forms: addresses, addressed. Frame: <bounded context> addresses <subdomain>.
- **play** (verb, state) → relation actor.plays. An actor of a bounded context's model is the one the product maps a product actor to there (decision 0141). Forms: plays, played. Frame: <actor> plays <product actor>.

# The product check over the jobs, the domain map and the capabilities

## Notes

Decisions 0140-0144. The product check, run by spec check in a shop that holds the product layer: each job assigned to exactly one bounded context and served by exactly one requirement group or more of one capability in it (0140); each of a job's actors, under its context's term, the subject of a trigger of a line of its serving groups or its trace (0141); a subdomain no bounded context addresses; each capability an entry point, a participant, a port, or an orphan reported (0144); a job's statement in its form. Needs b641 (serves:), b642 (the domain map) and b644 (the trace, for actors and participants).

## Requirements

### product

#### [g59] A job the product does not hold is not dropped
- [r59] If the person drops a job that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job.

#### [g60] A product actor the product does not hold is not dropped
- [r60] If the person drops a product actor that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the product actor.

#### [g61] A subdomain the product does not hold is not dropped
- [r61] If the person drops a subdomain that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the subdomain.

#### [g62] A bounded context the product does not hold is not dropped
- [r62] If the person drops a bounded context that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the bounded context.

#### [g63] A bounded context whose id is out of the context id form is not dropped
- [r63] If the person drops a bounded context whose id does not conform to the context id form, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the id.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation drop job(job)  outcomes: done → the job | refused: job a job needs, job a bounded context holds, job a subdomain serves, job outside the product
  operation drop actor(product actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product actor of a job, product actor a bounded context holds, product actor outside the product
  operation drop subdomain(subdomain)  outcomes: done → the subdomain | refused: subdomain a bounded context addresses, subdomain outside the product
  operation drop context(bounded context)  outcomes: done → the bounded context | refused: bounded context that holds a job, bounded context outside the product, id not in the context id form
```

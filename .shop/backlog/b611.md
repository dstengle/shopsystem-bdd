# The work-management context reaches the specification through a driven port

## Notes

batches reads and lays the specification's state directly (24 of its lines); a port in its own terms with specification's operations to lay, withdraw, check and generate for a proposal, and an in-process adapter. Part of the disentangling of this repository into hosted contexts (decisions 0129-0133).
Design: execution reaches the specification through a driven port of its own, the area laying (kind port), service laying with four operations in execution's terms: lay an item in a workspace, lift it from a workspace, check it there (done or failed), generate its scenarios there. The adapter wired in is the in-process one, which calls today's lay (backlog_lines.lay_onto, spec_corpus_write, batch_catalogues), lift (batch_start.unlay_lines, batch_parked.take_out), check (batch_start's violating rules) and generate (features generate); which adapter is wired is an adr of the batch (decision 0125), never a line. Technical calls: the port area is laying (kind port), its service the laying service, three operations in execution's terms: lay(item, workspace) done, lift(item, workspace) done, check(item, workspace) done | failed. ## Requirements

### laying

narrator: the batches service
group: service
purpose: The specification a batch works on, reached by the batches service through a driven port: an item laid in a workspace and lifted from it, the item checked there and its scenarios generated there, whichever adapter does it.
area: laying
kind: port

#### [g1] An item laid is held by the workspace
- [r1] While an item holds a requirement, when the batches service lays the item in a workspace, the laying service shall keep the workspace that holds the requirement and keep a scenario that refers to the requirement id of the requirement.
Relied on by: batches#r179

#### [g2] An item lifted is no longer held by the workspace
- [r2] While a workspace holds a requirement of an item, when the batches service lifts the item from the workspace, the laying service shall keep a workspace that does not hold the requirement.
Relied on by: batches#r182

#### [g3] A check of an item that violates a rule of severity error fails naming the rule
- [r3] While a requirement of an item violates a rule whose severity is `error`, when the batches service checks the item in a workspace, the laying service shall fail the request and name the rule.
Relied on by: batches#r180

#### [g4] A check of an item that violates no rule of severity error completes
- [r4] While an item holds no requirement that violates a rule whose severity is `error`, when the batches service checks the item in a workspace, the laying service shall complete the request.
Relied on by: batches#r180

### batches

retired: [r108, r106, r60]
retires: [r179 r108, r181 r106, r182 r60]

#### [g179] An item allocated to a batch is laid in the batch's workspace
- [r179] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item has no dependency on an item whose status is not `delivered` and the item does not exceed the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall allocate the item, keep a batch that holds the item, keep a batch plan of the batch and lay the item in the workspace of the batch.

#### [g180] An item the laying service has laid is checked in the workspace
- [r180] When the laying service completes the request to lay an item in a workspace, the batches service shall check the item in the workspace.

#### [g181] An item whose check the laying service fails is reopened naming the rules it violates
- [r181] When the laying service fails the request to check an item in a workspace, the batches service shall reopen the item and name every rule that a requirement of the item violates.

#### [g182] An item parked is lifted from the batch's workspace
- [r182] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and the workspace of the batch holds a requirement of the item, when a session parks the item, the batches service shall lift the item from the workspace of the batch.

#### [g183] An item the laying service has lifted is kept out in a change that cites it
- [r183] When the laying service completes the request to lift an item from a workspace, the batches service shall keep a kept change that cites the item.

#### [g184] An item whose check the laying service completes is kept in a change that cites it
- [r184] When the laying service completes the request to check an item in a workspace, the batches service shall keep a kept change that cites the item.

### laying model

```model
service laying
  operation lay(item, workspace)  outcomes: done
  operation lift(item, workspace)  outcomes: done
  operation check(item, workspace)  outcomes: done | failed
```

- **the laying service** (proper noun) → service laying. The specification a batch works on, as the batches service reaches it, whichever adapter lays, lifts and checks (decision 0125).
- **lay** (verb, action) → operation laying.lay. The batches service has an item's lines, model lines, terms and other pieces taken into the specification a workspace holds, and their scenarios generated there. Forms: lays, laid. Frame: <actor> lays <item> in <workspace>.
- **lift** (verb, action) → operation laying.lift. The batches service has what a lay of an item took into a workspace taken out of it again. Forms: lifts, lifted. Frame: <actor> lifts <item> from <workspace>.
- **check** (verb, action) → operation laying.check. The batches service has the requirements of an item laid in a workspace checked against the rules of severity error. Forms: checks, checked. Frame: <actor> checks <item> in <workspace>.

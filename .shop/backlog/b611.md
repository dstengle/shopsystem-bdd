# The work-management context reaches the specification through a driven port

## Notes

batches reads and lays the specification's state directly (24 of its lines); a port in its own terms with specification's operations to lay, withdraw, check and generate for a proposal, and an in-process adapter. Part of the disentangling of this repository into hosted contexts (decisions 0129-0133).
Design: execution reaches the specification through a driven port of its own, the area laying (kind port), service laying with four operations in execution's terms: lay an item in a workspace, lift it from a workspace, check it there (done or failed), generate its scenarios there. The adapter wired in is the in-process one, which calls today's lay (backlog_lines.lay_onto, spec_corpus_write, batch_catalogues), lift (batch_start.unlay_lines, batch_parked.take_out), check (batch_start's violating rules) and generate (features generate); which adapter is wired is an adr of the batch (decision 0125), never a line. Tracking and execution stay one context (decision 0133): this port is between work management and the specification. b611 is the port, its lines and the core lines of batches that request each operation and handle each outcome (v_pb1-v_pb4); the detail of what a lay keeps (batches' lines on the corpus, catalogues, index, model, vocabulary, packs, adapter contract) moves to the port's lines in later items.

### batches

retired: [r108, r106]
retires: [r170 r108, r172 r106]

#### [g170] An item allocated to a batch is laid in the batch's workspace
- [r170] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item has no dependency on an item whose status is not `delivered` and the item does not exceed the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall allocate the item, keep a batch that holds the item, keep a batch plan of the batch and lay the item in the workspace of the batch.

#### [g171] An item the laying service has laid is checked in the workspace
- [r171] When the laying service completes the request to lay an item in a workspace, the batches service shall check the item in the workspace.

#### [g172] An item whose check the laying service fails is reopened naming the rules it violates
- [r172] When the laying service fails the request to check an item in a workspace, the batches service shall reopen the item and name every rule that a requirement of the item violates.

#### [g173] An item whose check the laying service completes has its scenarios generated
- [r173] When the laying service completes the request to check an item in a workspace, the batches service shall generate the scenarios of the item in the workspace.

### laying model

```model
service laying
  operation lay(item, workspace)  outcomes: done
  operation lift(item, workspace)  outcomes: done
  operation check(item, workspace)  outcomes: done | failed
  operation generate(item, workspace)  outcomes: done
```

- **the laying service** (proper noun) → service laying. The specification a batch works on, as the batches service reaches it, whichever adapter lays, lifts, checks and generates (decision 0125).
- **lay** (verb, action) → operation laying.lay. The batches service has an item's lines, model lines, terms and other pieces taken into the specification a workspace holds. Forms: lays, laid. Frame: <actor> lays <item> in <workspace>.
- **lift** (verb, action) → operation laying.lift. The batches service has what a lay of an item took into a workspace taken out of it again. Forms: lifts, lifted. Frame: <actor> lifts <item> from <workspace>.
- **check** (verb, action) → operation laying.check. The batches service has the requirements of an item laid in a workspace checked against the rules of severity error. Forms: checks, checked. Frame: <actor> checks <item> in <workspace>.
- **generate** (verb, action) → operation laying.generate. The batches service has the scenarios of an item laid in a workspace written. Forms: generates, generated. Frame: <actor> generates the scenarios of <item> in <workspace>.

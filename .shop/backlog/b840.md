## Requirements

### checking

#### [g159] the check fails on a slot of a frame that names no kind, no actor and no parameter, naming the slot
- [r159] While a frame of a term has a slot and the slot refers to no term whose part of speech is `noun` and the slot refers to no term whose part of speech is `proper noun` and the slot refers to no parameter, if the person validates this specification, then the specification service shall fail the check and name the slot.

### specification model

```model
object frame kind of text
  relations: has → slot (many); ends → term (one)

object slot kind of text
  relations: refers to → term | parameter (many)
```

- **slot** (noun) → object slot. A word in angle brackets of a frame, which names the kind, the actor or the parameter that the frame takes there. Plural: slots.
- **end** (verb, state) → relation frame.ends. A frame is among the last sentences of its term's entry, so that no text but another frame follows it there. Forms: ends, ended. Frame: <frame> ends <term>.
- **refer** (verb, state) → relation slot.refers to. A slot of a frame names a term or a parameter between its angle brackets. Forms: refers, referred. Frame: <slot> refers to <term>. Frame: <slot> refers to <parameter>.

# An item retires an area that holds nothing

## Notes

Made in shaping b861 (quote-117: the laying area is retired; it owns no data): no item piece takes an area out of the domain model, so spec/model/laying.md (an empty model of kind port), its empty vocabulary layer spec/vocabulary/laying.md, execution's import of it and the area: and specifies: of the retired capability laying.md cannot be retired by b861 (spec forms: a domain model area has no command, b398). This item adds the piece beside --retire-model and --retire-term: work line <b> --retire-area <area> keeps the area in the item's retirements block, refused for an area the domain model does not hold, or one whose model holds a model line, whose layer holds a term, or of which a capability holds a live requirement; the apply takes out the area's model file and vocabulary layer, the area out of every other area's imports:, and area: and specifies: out of each capability of the area (each holds only retired ids), the Composition rewritten as the apply does. A --reinstate-area is left out (a follow-up idea if wanted).
Rating: medium-value, without it the laying area's empty model, layer and import stay in spec/ and b861 cannot retire them (quote-117), and any later emptied area has the same gap; medium-urgency, b861 (the last item of the laying retirement) waits on it; medium size, one new retirement kind beside --retire-model/--retire-term (backlog_retire, its apply in backlog_retire_lay, the capability frontmatter and other areas' imports) with 8 lines and their scenarios. Depends on b875, which rewrites backlog_retire_lay.

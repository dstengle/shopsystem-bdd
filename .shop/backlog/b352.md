# A pass/fail parse of lines: refused or not, without the hint

## Notes

The person, 2026-11-18 (quote-51): the handling of refused lines needs an optimized pass/fail option. The hint of a refused line (parsed up to, nearest words, may follow: spec_follow4 takes GF's completions and parses the prefix again with each candidate) cost most of the 6.5 minutes model parse took on 15 reworded lines, 10 of them refused (2026-11-18). Callers that need only whether a line reads (the readiness bar, work batch start's pick, model check, a session iterating over many lines) should get the verdict and the refusal's reason without the hint, and the hint only when asked for.

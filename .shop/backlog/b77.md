# The readiness bar refuses lines that share a trigger and a condition with another statement (v15n)

## Notes

From batch-11 (abandoned): b7 passed the readiness bar, but once its lines were laid on batch-11, test_revision_done's v15n check failed before any code was written. g20's r20 shares g17's trigger 'When the person runs features generate' and the check ignores the given block. The bar should run v15n on the laid lines, or v15n should tell groups apart by their given blocks. Deciding which is part of the shaping. b74 covers the same miss for v_t7.

Shaped 2026-11-16 (triage: shape, `.shop/records/2026-11-16-backlog-triage.md`; no quote names b77). Still current: `readiness_bar.well_formed` runs the capability reader, the lexicon, the parse, `model logic` and `features generate`, never `model.lp`'s v15n. Reproduced on a clone: an item with one line, in a new readiness group, repeating g25's While and trigger ("While an item is not `shaped`, if the person runs `work ready` on the item, ...") went `ready`; laid onto spec/, `model check` then printed `v15n two groups of a capability share features, condition and trigger (1)`. Decided: the bar runs v15n on the laid copy, and v15n is left as it is. In grammar 4 a group's condition is in its While and Where, and a given block holds only example data, so two groups of one condition and trigger are one scenario's lines and belong in one group (the skill's red flag "two lines with the same trigger and condition"). Modules: `readiness_bar.py` (after `_parses`, v15n read through `model`'s facts of the copy, as `model check` reads them; a reason for each pair with a group of the item, naming the group and `v15n`; a pair already on spec/ before the lay is not the item's), and its module-map row. The scenario: a shaped item whose one line shares its condition and trigger with a line of another group of its capability; `work ready` exits with an error naming the item's group and `v15n`. Needs the relation row `requirement group | in | item`. Left out: other model.lp rules the bar does not run (v_t7 was b74, withdrawn). Rating kept: high value (a batch built on such lines fails at its first `work batch done`), medium urgency, small.

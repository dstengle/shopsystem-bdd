# Parsing an item's lines takes seconds, not minutes, after its terms change

## Notes

The person, 2026-11-18 (quote-51). Measured 2026-11-18 on a scratch copy of the spec with decision-tiers' 15 lines reworded and 8 terms added: generating and compiling grammar 4 took 22 s, then model parse --file took about 6.5 minutes; the same file against the unchanged spec took 33 s. The parse cache is keyed by the grammar's sha, so any change of a context's terms misses every earlier parse of the context, and each line is parsed again with a 14 MB grammar. Measure where the time goes per line (GF's parse, spec_meaning's one meaning, the reading rules, the hint of a refused line: see the pass/fail item) and cut it. Related: b340 (work batch start's pick when items add terms), which this may settle.

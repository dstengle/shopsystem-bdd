# A contract's holds relation names only what a contract holds

## Notes

batch-90 review 1 observation: spec/model/specification.md declares contract holds term | design-decision, but no line puts design-decisions in a contract and contract_form writes none; take design-decision out of the relation, or add a line when a contract is to carry the decisions.
batch-90 review 1 observation: spec/model/specification.md declares that a contract holds design-decisions, which no line asks for and contract_form writes none of. Decided technically: the relation is taken out, no line added. A published contract carries the business rules a downstream context relies on (its requirements, terms, operations and their properties); a design-decision of tier judgement is the bounded context's own reasoning, read by its reviewer, and binds no downstream context. The item's model line restates the object contract without it, laid in place by b598.
Rated: low value (the model states a relation nothing uses), low urgency, small (one model line, laid by b598's restate).

# work ready of a ready item renumbers its ids past the ones a merged batch took, as the pick does, instead of naming them as appearing a second time

## Notes

Seen shaping b640: work ready b609, b641 and b645 (status ready) said g95/r95, g116/r116, g102/r102 appear a second time, the ids batch-98's merge took, while work batch start's pick renumbers them on a copy and passes them.
work ready (readiness_bar.check) lays the item's lines with their own ids, so an item whose group or requirement ids a merged batch already took in its capability is refused (spec_read: 'g120 appears a second time'), while work batch start's pick renumbers them first (backlog_renumber.renumber on a copy) and passes. The fix: the bar lays a renumbered copy of the item, as the pick does, writing nothing back to the item (its ids stay until the pick renumbers them). Scenario worlds: the item's group and requirement ids collide with ones its capability in spec/ holds (another line under the same id), so the scenario fails before the fix with the 'appears a second time' refusal; the item's file is checked unchanged after work ready.
Rated high value and urgency: ready items are refused for ids the pick would renumber, blocking readiness; small: the bar lays a renumbered copy.

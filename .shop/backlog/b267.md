# Ordered collections: a list ordered by its items' values or by an attribute of its items, its members and its order checkable and generated

## Notes

Reach: a collection kind whose items are ordered by their own value or by an attribute of each item, ascending or descending ('a video list that holds every video from the channel in order of publication time, newest first'); membership and order in the logical form and model logic (each pair of members ordered by the attribute's order, spec_lf_order); spec_steps4's Givens of two or more members with values in a known order and a Then that the list holds them in that order; coordinated relative clauses on one noun ('whose ... and that ...'). Related: b189 (newest and oldest), b146 (collections and counts, delivered), known limit #14 (ordered choice, rank order).
Reshaped under decision 0093 (requirements require standard operations, they do not define them), after the person's quote-29 (shape all four, fixes first, then ordering and comparisons), quote-30 and quote-31 and the review .shop/records/2026-11-15-collections-review.md (ordering does not parse, known limit #14). The note above predates 0093: its pairwise reading (each pair of members ordered through spec_lf_order, Givens printed as an expected sequence, 'newest first') is superseded and not built. The item builds: an order said 'ordered by <attribute>, in ascending order' or 'in descending order' after the noun phrase it orders, in two constructs: `ordered`, on a collection noun that holds members ('a batch that holds every item whose status is `ready`, ordered by score, in descending order'), and `ordered-every`, on a printed 'every K' of a response ('`migrate` shall print every upgrade step, ordered by release, in ascending order'); the attribute one of the member kind, found up the kind tree. Modules: grammar/Base.gf and spec_rgl_np (the order phrase on a noun phrase and on an every), a new spec_lf_ordered.py (one translation in each place a line is read: the form's one atom ordered(x, T, ascending|descending), x the collection or, of a printed every, the printed sequence of the every box; its line in model parse --form; the check's fact; spec_steps4's Givens and Then), spec_lf_facts and grammar/check.lp (ordered(R,X,T,Dir) a fact: two lines that require one collection, or one printing, in opposite orders on one trigger are an L1; nothing else proved, no pairs), spec_steps4 (Givens of three members with distinct values of the attribute in neither the order nor its reverse, e.g. 2, 3, 1; the generator never gives a tie; a Then 'the batch b1 is ordered by score, in descending order' or 'the printed items are ordered by ...'), features_vocab4 and a shared handler in tests/scenarios (a sorted comparison of the actual output, non-strict so ties pass), spec_lexicon and spec_value_kinds (an attribute entry's 'Type: number.', 'Type: time.' or 'Type: text.', whose values that type orders; today the lexicon has no value type at all, only named values with an optional Order:), spec_lf_order (an attribute of named values ordered by its Order: as today), spec_grammar4's refusal of an order on an attribute with neither a type nor an Order:, naming it; grammar/language.md (#14 narrowed), model_constructs_table and grammar/constructs.md (the codes `ordered` and `ordered-every`, their example context gaining 'score (attribute of item): Type: number.'). Left out: a then-by key, position, first and top n, uniqueness, type-flavoured words ('newest first'). Depends on b268, b272, b273.
Rated high value (ordering is the first standard operation decision 0093 names, and a consumer's latest-videos list waits on it), medium urgency (behind b268, b272 and b273), large (grammar, form, check, steps, a value type in the lexicon, a shared handler and the catalogue).

## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **order** (noun): "ordered by" an attribute and "in ascending order" or "in descending order" after a noun phrase, which a requirement that uses `ordered` or `ordered-every` requires of a collection or of what an agent prints, and which its logical form keeps as one atom `ordered(x, T, ascending)` or `ordered(x, T, descending)`. Kind of: thing.
- **value order** (noun): the order of an attribute's values: the order its entry's `Order:` gives, or the order of the type its entry's `Type:` declares (a number, a time or a text). Kind of: thing.
- **contradict** (verb, state): two orders of one collection, or of one printing, under one trigger and condition have opposite directions. Forms: contradicts, contradicted. Frame: order contradicts order.

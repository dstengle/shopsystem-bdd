# Ordered collections: a list ordered by its items' values or by an attribute of its items, its members and its order checkable and generated

## Notes

Reach: a collection kind whose items are ordered by their own value or by an attribute of each item, ascending or descending ('a video list that holds every video from the channel in order of publication time, newest first'); membership and order in the logical form and model logic (each pair of members ordered by the attribute's order, spec_lf_order); spec_steps4's Givens of two or more members with values in a known order and a Then that the list holds them in that order; coordinated relative clauses on one noun ('whose ... and that ...').

# Unlaying one of two items that retire lines of one group puts back only its own retired line

## Notes

batch-138 t3 (b750) found: when two items of a batch retire two lines of one group, the lay removes the whole group; unlaying one item (park) restores the whole group from git, the other item's retired line included, and the park fails with 'features generate of work-queue failed: work-queue.md: 21: r2 is retired'. Cause: backlog_retires.unlay (scripts/backlog_retires.py:160-169). Source: batch-138 t3 report.
The fix: backlog_retires.unlay (scripts/backlog_retires.py, the group put back whole) puts back only the item's own retired lines when the group's other retired lines are retired by another item still laid: a group missing from the workspace is put back holding only the rids being unlaid, the lines of it another item retires left out (its retired: and the other item's retires: kept). One port line of laying, relied on by batches#r182 (a park lifts the item). World that fails first: laying_handlers' workspace with two items laid that retire r1 and r2 of one group of a capability (the group removed whole by the lay), the lift of the first item requested: today the group comes back whole, r2 with it, and the lift is refused ('features generate ... r2 is retired'), so the Then that the workspace does not hold r2 fails. Modules: scripts/backlog_retires.py, tests/scenarios/laying_handlers.py (or a laying_handlers_retire.py beside it), tests/scripts/test_backlog_retires*.py. Source: batch-138 t3 report (b750).

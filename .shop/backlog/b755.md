# Unlaying one of two items that retire lines of one group puts back only its own retired line

## Notes

batch-138 t3 (b750) found: when two items of a batch retire two lines of one group, the lay removes the whole group; unlaying one item (park) restores the whole group from git, the other item's retired line included, and the park fails with 'features generate of work-queue failed: work-queue.md: 21: r2 is retired'. Cause: backlog_retires.unlay (scripts/backlog_retires.py:160-169). Source: batch-138 t3 report.

## Requirements

### laying

#### [g5] A lift of an item keeps out a requirement of the same group that another requirement in the workspace retires
- [r5] While an item holds a requirement that retires the requirement id of a requirement of a requirement group and a workspace holds another requirement that retires the requirement id of another requirement of the requirement group, when the batches service lifts the item from the workspace, the laying service shall keep the workspace that does not hold the requirement.

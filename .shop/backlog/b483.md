# work batch start splits a batch whose items' change would pass the reviewer's 1000-line limit

## Notes

work batch start (scripts/batch_pick.py) picks up to batch_size ready items with no regard to the size of their change, while the pull request review reads a change of at most 1000 lines (batch_brief.SIZE); the last three batches were about 2800-2900 lines. The pick estimates each picked item's change from its size rating, its requirement lines and its model lines, and stops before the batch's estimate passes the review limit; the first item picked is taken whatever its estimate, so a pick never comes back empty. The person's words in quote-68.
Rated high-value: a batch of 2800-2900 lines is read by a reviewer whose brief stops at 1000, so most of each change goes unreviewed; medium-urgency: every batch until then is too big, but batches still merge; medium size: an estimate per item in batch_pick, the stop in the pick loop, a scenario handler.

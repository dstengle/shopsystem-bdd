### registry model

```model
service contract registry
  operation register(contract, adapter spec, provider, version)  compensated by deregister  outcomes: done → the contract | refused: contract not registered
  operation deregister(contract, adapter spec, provider, version)  outcomes: done → the contract
```

- **deregister** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.deregister. The specification service has the contract registry undo a registration of a contract for an adapter spec, so that the registry no longer keeps the contract for it. Forms: deregisters, deregistered. Frame: <actor> deregisters <contract> for <adapter spec>.

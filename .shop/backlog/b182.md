# R2 does not bind "the K" to a referent of a closed no/every/only box

## Notes

batch-26 review 1 (b139): spec_lf_read.the takes referents made by "no K"/"every K" (spec_lf_np.det, st.ref) as candidates; e.g. "While a session has no worktree, ... shall print the worktree." binds to the worktree under not[]. Leave out referents whose quant is no/every/only once their box is closed.

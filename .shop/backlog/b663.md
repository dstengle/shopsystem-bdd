# work batch start leaves an item past the review limit ready even when it fails the readiness bar, as batches#r101 does not yet say

## Notes

Raised by batch-104's review (f2, observation on b640): b640 checks the review limit before the readiness bar, so a ready item past the limit that fails the bar is left out ready, never reopened; batches#r101 says the batches service reopens any ready item that fails the bar. Retire batches#r101 with a line limited to items within the review limit, and take a line for the item past it if a scenario fails before the fix.

## Requirements

### batches

retired: [r101, r107]
retires: [r130 r101, r131 r107]

#### [g130] A ready item within the review limit that no longer meets the bar is reopened
- [r130] While an item is `ready` and the item does not meet the readiness bar and the item does not exceed the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall reopen the item.

#### [g131] A ready item whose change would take the batch past the review limit is not allocated, whether or not it meets the bar
- [r131] While an item is `ready` and the item exceeds the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall not allocate the item.

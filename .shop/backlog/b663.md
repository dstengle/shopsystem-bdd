# work batch start leaves an item past the review limit ready even when it fails the readiness bar, as batches#r101 does not yet say

## Notes

Raised by batch-104's review (f2, observation on b640): b640 checks the review limit before the readiness bar, so a ready item past the limit that fails the bar is left out ready, never reopened; batches#r101 says the batches service reopens any ready item that fails the bar. Retire batches#r101 with a line limited to items within the review limit, and take a line for the item past it if a scenario fails before the fix.

# A requirement group serves a job

## Notes

Decision 0140. Technical calls: serves: <job> written on a requirement group through an item (drafting: work line <b> --serves <cap>#g<n> <job>, and on a new group with --group), laid by work batch start into the capability's group, and read by spec check as the group's serves fact. That the job exists and is assigned to this context is the product check's (b643). Part of the product layer of a compact shop (b602).
Rated: high value (serves: on a group is what the product check b643 and the trace b644 read), medium urgency (b643 waits on it), medium size (three lines and an operation: work line's option, the lay of serves: into the capability's group, spec check's serves fact from groups; about 450 changed lines).

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g66] A requirement group dedicated to a job serves the job in the item
- [r66] When the person dedicates a requirement group to a job with an item, the drafting service shall keep the item that holds the requirement group that serves the job.

### batches

#### [g116] A batch opened keeps the job a requirement group of an item serves
- [r116] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item holds a requirement group that serves a job, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall keep a capability that has a requirement group that serves the job.

### checking

#### [g102] A capability whose requirement group serves a job is not unserved
- [r102] While this bounded context has a job and a requirement group of a capability serves the job, when the person validates this specification, the specification service shall return no capability that violates `w_unserved_cap`.

### specification model

```model
object requirement group
  relations: serves → job (many)
```

- **serve** (verb, state) → relation requirement group.serves. A requirement group names a job of the product as one its capability coordinates, so that the job is traced through the group. Forms: serves, served. Frame: <requirement group> serves <job>.

### tracking model

```model
service drafting
  operation dedicate(requirement group, job, item)  outcomes: done
```

- **dedicate** (verb, action) → operation drafting.dedicate. Forms: dedicates, dedicated. Frame: <actor> dedicates <requirement group> to <job>.

# spec job and spec plays check the product actor first

## Notes

New-user trial 2 of 2026-11-26 (quote-104): spec job x1 --statement 'When ..., the member wants ...' with no --actor declared with actors = []; with --actor 'the reader' (no product actor) the refusal blames the statement; spec plays 'the reader' --as 'the member' reports --as, never that 'the reader' is no product actor, and does not say the actor is on a batch not yet merged.
New-user trial 2 (quote-104): the product actor is checked before the rest. A job declared with no product actor is refused naming the product actor (r14, which kept such a job with no actor, retired); a job whose product actor is outside the product names the product actor (r5) and no longer the statement (r43 narrowed to a product actor and a mentioned actor both in the product); a product actor mapped that is outside the product names the product actor (r25) and no longer the actor (r40 narrowed to a product actor in the product). No model change: each refusal names a parameter whose reason product.declare job and product.map declare already.
Rated medium value (refusals that name the wrong parameter mislead a new user but have a workaround), high urgency (found in the trial the person asked to re-run), small (three narrowed refusals in product_layer and product_assign).
renumbered by work batch start: product#g59 -> product#g62, product#r59 -> product#r62

## Requirements

### product

retired: [r14, r43, r40]
retires: [r62 r14, r60 r43, r61 r40]

#### [g62] A job declared without a product actor is refused
- [r62] If the person declares a job with a job statement and without a product actor, then the product service shall refuse the request.

#### [g60] A job whose statement mentions another actor of the product than the job's is refused
- [r60] If the person declares a job with a product actor that is in the product and a job statement that mentions a different product actor that is in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job statement.

#### [g61] A product actor of the product is not mapped to an actor the domain model does not hold
- [r61] If the person maps a product actor that is in the product with this bounded context and an actor that the domain model does not hold, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the actor.

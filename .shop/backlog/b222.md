# Grammar 4: "the K" never binds to a referent inside a no or not box

## Notes

Found writing b158 (2026-11-10): in "While an item is `ready` and the item passes the readiness bar and the item has no dependency on an item whose status is not `delivered`, when the person runs `work batch start`, `work` shall pick the item, ...", `model parse --form` binds the response's "the item" to x4, the item inside the not box of "no dependency on an item", not to x1. spec_lf_read.the() takes the latest referent whose origin is accessible and ignores the box it was made in; R2 in grammar/language.md says "an earlier referent", and a referent under no/not (or every/only) is not accessible outside its box (DRS accessibility). batches#r20 of b158 waits on this.

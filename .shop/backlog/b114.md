# The check reads every, only, at most one and a negated indefinite as the Gherkin generator does

## Notes

Found by b112's research, phase 1 (.shop/records/2026-11-08-cnl-research-phase-1/our-cnl.md §2.2, §3, §6): spec_facts produces facts for every on an object, only and count that grammar/check.lp never reads, while spec_steps reads every; 'at most one' and 'one' mean the same as 'a'; 'does not hold a K' reads as some K not held, unlike 'holds no K'. A scenario can test what the check never saw. b112 may replace the readings wholesale (a DRS-like form); until then the two should agree, or a line using a construct the check ignores should be refused or warned.

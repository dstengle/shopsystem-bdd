# Rewording a term on an item takes its bullet as given and --into as --term does

## Notes

New-user trial of 2026-11-26 (quote-103): an entry starting '- ' as a positional is taken for an option ('unrecognized arguments: - **the housework service** ...') and --help does not say to put -- first; --reword-term refuses --into ('--into only of a model line') while --reword-model needs it and --term takes it.
New-user trial of 2026-11-26, the person's words in quote-103 and quote-104: --reword-term takes an entry opening with '- ' as an option unless -- comes first, and refuses --into, which --term and --reword-model take.

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g92] Rewording a term of an item in a capability keeps the new term
- [r92] When the person rewords a term in an item with a capability, the drafting service shall keep a different term in the item.

### tracking model

```model
service drafting
  operation reword term(term, item, capability)  outcomes: done | refused: term not in the item
```

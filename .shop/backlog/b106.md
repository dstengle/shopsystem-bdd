# A prepositional verb's Frame: reads (viewer subscribes to channel), and a Frame: that does not read says why

## Notes

Fix of the defect against b82 (.shop/batches/13/defect-*.md; feed's 3.0 upgrade report finding 4). The design's call 1 gives 'Frame: user subscribes to channel.' as a prepositional verb whose preposition is part of the verb; spec_frames.frame reads 'to channel' as the object. Lines use the verb with its preposition ('a viewer subscribes to a channel', 'a caller asks for a video list'). A Frame: field present but unreadable gets its own message naming the word it could not read, not v_t11's 'gives no Frame:'.
Design call 1 of .shop/records/2026-11-07-verb-frames/design.md: in 'Frame: viewer subscribes to channel.' the preposition belongs to the verb (the verb term stays 'subscribe'; no 'subscribe to' term), and a line says it as 'a viewer subscribes to a channel'; the typed verb funs (spec_typed_verbs) carry the preposition before the object, in triggers, conditions, verb phrases and steps. A Frame: that is present but does not read gets its own v_t-code, apart from v_t11, whose message names the word it could not read and the kinds it expected. feed's report (finding 4) asks this, the person's paste in quote-16; feed's suggestion of the preposition in the term is not taken, as the design settled it in the frame.
Rated high value (a consumer's correct glossary fails model check), high urgency (a defect of 3.0 that feed met), medium size (the frame reader, typed verb funs with a preposition, a new v_t-code).

## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **prepositional** (adjective of term): a verb whose frame puts a preposition between the verb and its object, as in `Frame: viewer subscribes to channel.`.
- **prepositioned** (adjective of requirement): a requirement with a prepositional verb followed by its preposition and its object, as in `a viewer subscribes to a channel`.
- **misframed** (adjective of term): a verb whose glossary entry has a `Frame:` that does not read as its subject, the verb and the kinds the verb takes.

#### [g68] `model check` reads a prepositional verb's frame
- [r68] When the person runs `model check` on a term that is prepositional, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g69] `model parse` parses a prepositional verb with its preposition
- [r69] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is prepositioned, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g70] `model check` refuses a frame that does not read
- [r70] If the person runs `model check` on a term that is misframed, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the term.

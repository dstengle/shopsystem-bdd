# A prepositional verb's Frame: reads (viewer subscribes to channel), and a Frame: that does not read says why

## Notes

Fix of the defect against b82 (.shop/batches/13/defect-*.md; feed's 3.0 upgrade report finding 4). The design's call 1 gives 'Frame: user subscribes to channel.' as a prepositional verb whose preposition is part of the verb; spec_frames.frame reads 'to channel' as the object. Lines use the verb with its preposition ('a viewer subscribes to a channel', 'a caller asks for a video list'). A Frame: field present but unreadable gets its own message naming the word it could not read, not v_t11's 'gives no Frame:'.

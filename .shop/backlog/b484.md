# work merge refuses a successor that is the item itself or closed, as its stated refusals

## Requirements

### items

retired: [r33]
retires: [r41 r33]

#### [g41] An open item merged into a successor is withdrawn
- [r41] While a successor exists and the status of an item is one of `idea`, `shaped`, `ready` or `needs-input`, when the person merges the item with the successor and a reason, the backlog service shall set the status of the item to `withdrawn` and keep the item.

#### [g42] An item is not merged into a closed successor
- [r42] While the status of a successor is one of `delivered`, `refused` or `withdrawn` and the status of an item is one of `idea`, `shaped`, `ready` or `needs-input`, if the person merges the item with the successor and a reason, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and name the successor.

#### [g43] An item is not merged into itself
- [r43] If the person merges a successor with the successor and a reason, then the backlog service shall refuse the request and name the successor.

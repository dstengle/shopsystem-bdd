# order-by on a collection: the form orders the collection, the scenario its members

## Notes

From the release 0.36.0 review (finding 3): for 'print a worktree that holds a file, ordered by version, in descending order' the form orders the worktree (ordered(x4 worktree, version)) while the generated scenario gives the files' versions and checks their order. One of the two is wrong: settle where order-by on a collection puts the order (decision 0093: the collection's members) and make the form say it.

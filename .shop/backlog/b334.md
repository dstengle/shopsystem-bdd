# order-by on a collection: the form orders the collection, the scenario its members

## Notes

From the release 0.36.0 review (finding 3): for 'print a worktree that holds a file, ordered by version, in descending order' the form orders the worktree (ordered(x4 worktree, version)) while the generated scenario gives the files' versions and checks their order. One of the two is wrong: settle where order-by on a collection puts the order (decision 0093: the collection's members) and make the form say it.

## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **collection** (noun): a thing that a noun phrase of a requirement names and that holds members by a relation row whose word is `in`, as a worktree holds files. Kind of: thing.
- **member** (noun): a thing that a collection holds by a relation row whose word is `in`, as a file is a member of a worktree; an order said after a collection is an order of its members. Kind of: thing.

| order | of | member |  |
| member | of | collection |  |

#### [g323] `model parse --form` puts an order after a collection on its members
- [r323] When the person runs `model parse` with the `--form` option on a requirement that uses `order-by`, `model` shall print a logical form that uses an order of a member of a collection.

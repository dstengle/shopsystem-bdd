# A line drafted into a capability new or recodified on its item can retire a requirement of another

## Notes

Found by batch-176's review (b882): work line --into <cap> --retires <other cap>#r<n> is refused unless --into names a codified-2 capability file of the spec, so a capability the item adds (--capability) or recodifies cannot take a restated line.

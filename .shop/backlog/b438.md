# Grammar 4's Context compiled with --optimize-pgf, its sequence table re-sorted case-sensitively, and every subtree linearized from the whole tree

## Notes

Research 2026-11-20 (option B): --optimize-pgf parses 24-31x faster (labels 39,207 -> 6,113, PGF 13 -> 2.3 MB) but GF 3.12 loses 25% of lines: PGF/Optimize.hs sorts the sequence table case-insensitively unless the flag case_sensitive is 'on', which the compiler never writes, while the C runtime searches it case-sensitively. A build-time re-sort gives 0 differences on 413 live and 105 mutated lines. Before it ships: spec_grammar4._words and spec_one_thing.refusal (and features_vocab4's 8 sites before ContextSteps is ever optimized) take words from bracketedLinearize of the whole tree, since linearizing a subtree aborts the process (SIGABRT, linearizer.c:925); a guard on the PGF version and the order; the option in the cache sha; the bug reported to gf-core and the re-sort dropped once a fixed release is pinned.

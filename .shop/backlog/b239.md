# Grammar 4 caches its sources, its grammar and its parses

## Notes

Profiling readiness_bar.check on b154 (2026-11-10): 21 s for one reword item of 37 lines. About 10 s is scripts/spec_grammar4.py recomputing, sources called 150 times (6.5 s) and load 114 times (7.8 s) for 76 parses, nothing kept per process; about 6 s is two GF compiles (spec_grammar._compile). Grammar 3 has the on-disk parse cache scripts/spec_parse_cache.py keyed by the grammar's sources, the grammar, the lexicon's words and the text; grammar 4 has none. To make true: grammar 4's sources and loaded grammar computed once per process for one lexicon (memoised, invalidated when the lexicon or packs change); grammar 4's parses through the on-disk parse cache like grammar 3's, keyed so a change to the grammar, lexicon or text misses; the compiled PGF reused while its sources are unchanged; results identical to today's. ## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **parse cache** (noun): the files in which `model parse` keeps a line's parse, keyed by its grammar's sources, its grammar, its lexicon's words and its text. Plural: parse caches. Kind of: file.
- **compiled grammar** (noun): the PGF file a context's grammar compiles to from its sources. Plural: compiled grammars. Kind of: file.
- **reparsed** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, that `model parse` has parsed before, with its grammar's sources, its lexicon's words and its packs unchanged since.
- **outdated** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, that `model parse` has parsed before, with a term of its lexicon or a pack changed since.

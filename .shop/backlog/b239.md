## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **parse cache** (noun): the files in which `model parse` keeps a line's parse, keyed by its grammar's sources, its grammar, its lexicon's words and its text. Plural: parse caches. Kind of: file.
- **compiled grammar** (noun): the PGF file a context's grammar compiles to from its sources. Plural: compiled grammars. Kind of: file.
- **reparsed** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, that `model parse` has parsed before, with its grammar's sources, its lexicon's words and its packs unchanged since.
- **outdated** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, that `model parse` has parsed before, with a term of its lexicon or a pack changed since.
- **uncached** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, that `model parse` has not parsed before, of a grammar whose sources are unchanged since its compiled grammar was written.

#### [g174] `model parse` reads a codified-2 line parsed before from the parse cache
- [r174] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is reparsed, `model` shall read a parse cache, write no compiled grammar and exit successfully.

#### [g175] `model parse` parses a codified-2 line again once its lexicon or a pack changed
- [r175] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is outdated, `model` shall write a parse cache and exit successfully.

#### [g176] `model parse` reuses the compiled grammar of unchanged sources
- [r176] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is uncached, `model` shall use a compiled grammar, write no compiled grammar and exit successfully.

## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **restricting** (adjective of capability): a capability with a line that obliges an agent to change only things of one kind, and a line in which the agent changes a thing of that kind.
- **exhaustive** (adjective of capability): a capability with a line that obliges a tool to print every thing of a kind and a line that forbids it to print a thing of that kind, under one trigger and state.

#### [g71] `model logic` finds no clash between only and a change of that kind
- [r71] When the person runs `model logic` on a capability that is restricting, `model` shall not print "L1".

#### [g72] `model logic` finds the clash of every and a forbidden print
- [r72] When the person runs `model logic` on a capability that is exhaustive, `model` shall print "L1".

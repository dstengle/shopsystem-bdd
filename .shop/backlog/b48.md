## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g28] A universal condition removed from an item is no longer kept
- [r28] When the person removes a universal condition from an item, the drafting service shall not keep the universal condition in the item.

#### [g29] An upstream entry removed from an item is no longer kept
- [r29] When the person removes an upstream entry from an item, the drafting service shall not keep the upstream entry in the item.

#### [g30] A given block removed from an item is no longer kept
- [r30] When the person removes a given block from an item, the drafting service shall not keep the given block in the item.

#### [g31] Removing a universal condition that the item does not hold is refused
- [r31] If the person removes a universal condition from an item that does not hold the universal condition, then the drafting service shall refuse the request and name the universal condition.

### tracking model

```model
service drafting
  operation take out(item, universal condition, upstream entry, given block)  outcomes: done | refused: universal condition not in the item, upstream entry not in the item, given block not in the item
  operation replace(item, universal condition, upstream entry, given block)  outcomes: done | refused: universal condition not in the item, upstream entry not in the item, given block not in the item
```

- **remove** (verb, action) → operation drafting.take out. Forms: removes, removed. Frame: <actor> removes <universal condition> from <item>. Frame: <actor> removes <upstream entry> from <item>. Frame: <actor> removes <given block> from <item>.
- **replace** (verb, action) → operation drafting.replace. Forms: replaces, replaced. Frame: <actor> replaces <universal condition> in <item>. Frame: <actor> replaces <upstream entry> in <item>. Frame: <actor> replaces <given block> in <item>.

# The batch brief tells the driver to wait on its subagents in the foreground

## Notes

batch-96's driver dispatched its task subagents in the background and ended its turn twice while t2 ran; the subagent's completion reached the starting session, not the driver, and the batch sat about an hour (wall time 1 h 24 min, $26.18). This repository's [batch] rules now carry the rule; the plugin's own brief (batch_driver.py) should say it for every consumer.

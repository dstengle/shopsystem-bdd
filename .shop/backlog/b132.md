## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **pack** (noun): a file of terms a bounded context imports: `grammar/packs/<name>.md` of the plugin, or `spec/packs/<name>.md` of the bounded context. Kind of: file.
- **importing** (adjective of bounded context): a bounded context whose `spec/index.md` imports a pack.
- **misimporting** (adjective of bounded context): a bounded context whose `spec/index.md` imports a pack that neither the plugin nor the bounded context has.
- **unmarked** (adjective of term): a term that a pack the bounded context imports defines too, whose entry has no `Redefines:`.
- **redefining** (adjective of term): a term whose entry has `Redefines:` naming a pack that defines it.

#### [g71] `model lexicon` shows the terms of the imported packs
- [r71] When the person runs `model lexicon` on a bounded context that is importing, `model` shall print every term and exit successfully.

#### [g72] `model check` refuses an import of no pack
- [r72] If the person runs `model check` on a bounded context that is misimporting, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the pack.

#### [g73] `model check` refuses a pack's term redefined without Redefines:
- [r73] If the person runs `model check` on a term that is unmarked, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the term.

#### [g74] `model check` passes a term marked Redefines:
- [r74] When the person runs `model check` on a term that is redefining, `model` shall exit successfully.

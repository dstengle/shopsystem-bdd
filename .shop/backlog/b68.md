# A request's issue is never left out of step with its record

## Notes

From .shop/batches/9/review-1.md f5 (and the first, refused reply's second observation): after b36, work request leaves an orphaned issue and a record with no issue: when the commit of the issue's URL fails after open_issue succeeded; work fits/resolve leave the record delivered/refused while the issue stays open when gh issue close fails after the URL commit; a failed second commit leaves a posted comment's URL unrecorded. Hardening: close before the second commit, print the posted URL on a failed second commit, or let a re-run find the issue it opened.

Narrowed after batch-9 review 3 (f6, the person's comment on PR #24): f6's fix names a posted comment's or opened issue's URL when the commit of it fails, and a re-run or `work sync` records it without posting again, so the orphaned issue and the unrecorded comment URL are done. Left: `work fits` and `work resolve` leave the record delivered or refused while the issue stays open when `gh issue close` fails.
Merged in from b70 in the 2026-11-16 triage: a failing close in request_post.committed's finally (143-145) replaces the not_recorded refusal: a second case of the close failing.

# The capability reading renamed parsing

## Notes

The person (2026-11-19): reading implies writing and is usually the beginning IO operation in a pipeline; parsing names what the language service does. Its group titles still name `model parse`, a CLI mechanism, and get business titles in the same change.
The person's words are in this item's notes (2026-11-19). Shaped with b370's approach (quote-57): a new capability parsing (area specification, the language service) takes reading's place; its lines are the language service's general behaviour (a requirement conforming to the language reference parsed and its logical form returned, a requirement not in the language, of two meanings, breaking a reading rule or using a construct the construct catalogue does not hold refused naming why, a fragment refused with the terms that may follow, the corpus's form returned, the parse cache and compiled grammar reused), group titles in business terms (no model parse). Every reading line is retired (the construct-by-construct ones with no line, the general ones restated in parsing). For the build: the emptied spec/capabilities/reading.md and its feature, tests and handlers are removed or git mv'd to parsing; the reading rules R1-R12 are named by the language reference (grammar/language.md).

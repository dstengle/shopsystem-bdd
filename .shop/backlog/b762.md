# spec coupling tells a word two areas each define from a word one borrows from the other

## Notes

spec coupling (b612) on this repository: tracking by specification counts 15 words and 293 requirements, mostly words both areas define (status, import, retire, product: the two-kinds rule), which are homonyms, not reliance; report them apart from a borrowed word so the coupling figures guide the split.

#### [g147] the coupling report names the words one area borrows for another area's elements
- [r147] While an area holds an element and another area holds a term that means the element and the term has a word, when the person reports on the shop, the specification service shall return a coupling of the area that holds the word.

object homonym kind of text

- **homonym** (noun) → object homonym. A word that two areas each define for an element of their own, so that neither relies on the other for it. Plural: homonyms.

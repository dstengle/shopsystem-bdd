# A scenario fails when the world it is given leaves one of its Givens unread

## Notes

Found shaping b877: design_model_check_worlds' build takes the first world whose facts are a subset of the scenario's, so a scenario whose extra Given (an item holds a term) no world reads falls through to a broader world (CACHE.held) that ignores it, and passes vacuously. As scenario_unread does for a Given no world reads at all (b748), a world must account for every fact of its scenario, or the scenario fails naming the unread fact. Audit the other world tables (feature_file_tools, laying/applying WORLDS) for the same subset match.

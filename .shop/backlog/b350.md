# Grammar 4's caches stay bounded: compiled grammars and parses unused for a while are pruned

## Notes

The person, 2026-11-18 (quote-51): prune the cache and handle that ongoing. Found 2026-11-18: ~/.cache/shopsystem-bdd/ held 7.2 GB of compiled grammars (pgf/, about 5,100, one per set of grammar 4's sources, 14 MB each) and 1.3 GB of parses (parse/, keyed by the grammar's sha): every change of a context's terms leaves a new grammar and its parses behind, and nothing removes them. The cache is shared by every context and plugin version on the machine (another session's context used it at the time). Pruned by hand that day, keeping each grammar read in the last 48 hours and its parses: 820 MB left. Wanted: the plugin keeps it bounded itself (by age of last use, by size, or both), safe while other processes read it.

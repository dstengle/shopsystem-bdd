# An L1 whose clash goes through a relation row with no cardinality names the row and says that 'one' makes it functional

## Notes

feed's 3.0 upgrade report finding 1: r5 and r6 clash on a video from both a subscribed and an unsubscribed channel because 'video | from | channel' has no cardinality. A relation row's fourth cell 'one' already makes it functional (spec_logic_vocab.functional, fun/1 in check.lp), so feed can write '| video | from | channel | one |'; the report's authors did not find it, and model logic's finding gave no hint. Also weigh adding 'from' to the prepositions read as functional with no cardinality.

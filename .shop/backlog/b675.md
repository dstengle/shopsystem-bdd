# The product's domain map: each job assigned to one bounded context, and each product actor mapped to a context's actor

## Notes

Split from b642 for size (decisions 0140, 0141). Lines product#r8-r11 (renumbered when laid). Technical calls: product.assign (spec assign <job> --to <context>) and product.map (spec plays <product actor> --in <context> --as <actor>), kept in the product layer; a job is assigned once (a job a bounded context holds is refused, naming the job; reassignment with history is b676s). How the worlds must be built so each scenario fails before: product_handlers writes the product file with the job, the product actor and the bounded context (b642s domain map) the Givens describe, a bounded context holding the job when the While says so, a product actor outside the product when the Given says it is not in it; runs the command with the instance values; the keep Thens read the assignment or the mapping (the product actor under the context, called by the actor) back from the kept product file; the refusals check an error exit, nothing committed and the value and option named. Before the fix spec assign and spec plays do not exist, so every scenario fails.

## Requirements

### product

#### [g8] A job is assigned to the bounded context that serves it
- [r8] When the person assigns a job to a bounded context, the product service shall keep the job in the bounded context and complete the request.

#### [g9] A job assigned to a bounded context is not assigned again
- [r9] While a bounded context holds a job, if the person assigns the job to a bounded context, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job.

#### [g10] A product actor is mapped to the actor a bounded context calls it by
- [r10] When the person maps a product actor with a bounded context and an actor, the product service shall keep the product actor in the bounded context and complete the request.

#### [g11] A product actor the product does not hold is not mapped
- [r11] If the person maps a product actor that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the product actor.

### specification model

```model
object bounded context
  relations: holds → job | product actor (many)

object product actor
  relations: is called → actor (many)

service product
  operation assign(job, bounded context)  outcomes: done → the job | refused: job assigned already
  operation map(product actor, bounded context, actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product actor outside the product
```

- **assign** (verb, action) → operation product.assign. The person gives a job of the product to the one bounded context whose capabilities serve it (decision 0140). Forms: assigns, assigned. Frame: <actor> assigns <job> to <bounded context>.
- **map** (verb, action) → operation product.map. The person says which actor of a bounded context's model plays a product actor there (decision 0141). Forms: maps, mapped. Frame: <actor> maps <product actor>.

# The product's domain map: each job assigned to one bounded context, and each product actor mapped to a context's actor

## Requirements

### specification model

```model
object bounded context
  relations: holds → job | product actor (many)

object product actor
  relations: is called → actor (many)

service product
  operation assign(job, bounded context)  outcomes: done → the job | refused: job assigned already, bounded context outside the product
  operation map(product actor, bounded context, actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product actor outside the product, bounded context outside the product
```

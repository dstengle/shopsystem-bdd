# An adapter spec speaks its provider's words

## Notes

C-6 of the contracts design, first slice. A pinned published contract becomes the adapter spec's provider area and provider capabilities, with a rename map for words and elements that clash with the port's (product becomes stocked product); an assumed provider description written by hand with assumed: true (decisions 0117, 0120).
Shaped 2026-11-25 (decisions 0117, 0120, 0122, 0123): four checking lines of the specification service validating an adapter spec: a line of the adapter spec reads a term of a provider's contract it holds, a provider's term that clashes with another term is renamed or refused, and an assumed description of a provider is accepted while each of its lines has a scenario. Wording after 0129/0133: the provider's words and lines are kept in the adapter spec, nested in its bounded context's spec, not a provider area of the parent's model. Its lines use b540's words (adapter spec, provider) and b348's (a contract holds a term), so work ready passes only once those are laid: b282 (an item checked over the new terms of the items it depends on) or their delivery. Rated high value (C-6, the adapter's provider words), medium urgency, medium size.

## Requirements

### checking

#### [g73] A line of an adapter spec reads a term of its provider's contract
- [r73] While an adapter spec holds a contract and a requirement of the adapter spec uses a term that the contract holds, when the person validates the adapter spec, the specification service shall complete the request.

#### [g74] A provider's term that clashes with another term is read under its new word
- [r74] While an adapter spec holds a contract that holds a term and the term clashes with another term and the adapter spec renames the term, when the person validates the adapter spec, the specification service shall complete the request.

#### [g75] A provider's term that clashes with another term and is not renamed is refused
- [r75] While an adapter spec holds a contract that holds a term and the term clashes with another term and the adapter spec does not rename the term, if the person validates the adapter spec, then the specification service shall fail the check and name the term.

### specification model

```model
object adapter spec
  relations: has → requirement (many); renames → term (many); holds → assumed description (many)

object assumed description kind of artifact
  relations: has → provider (one); holds → requirement id | term (many)

object term
  relations: clashes with → term (many)
```

- **assumed description** (noun) → object assumed description. A description of a provider that is no published contract, written by hand with `assumed: true` and evidence only when its scenarios run against the provider (decision 0120). Plural: assumed descriptions.
- **rename** (verb, state) → relation adapter spec.renames. An adapter spec gives a term of a provider's contract another word in the adapter spec, as `product` becomes `stocked product`. Forms: renames, renamed. Frame: <adapter spec> renames <term>.
- **clash** (verb, state) → relation term.clashes with. A term of a provider's contract is spelled as another term that the adapter spec reads and names another element. Forms: clashes, clashed. Frame: <term> clashes with <term>.

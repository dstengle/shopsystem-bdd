# A verb names an operation by each of its frames, so 'list a checklist' and 'list the checklists' are two requests of one word

## Notes

UX trial 2 (2026-11-20, 0.40.0): the person asked to 'list all my checklists' after 'list' already named checklists.list (the items of one checklist); since a vocabulary entry names one element, the session coined the verb 'browse' (checklists.browse, 'When the person browses, ...') and the CLI got a 'browse' command the person never said. A word of two frames naming two operations (v_t15 allows two senses whose kinds differ) or a guide in shaping-items on naming a second operation would keep the person's word.
Design .shop/records/2026-11-20-b420-one-verb-several-operations/design.md and design-decision 0111 (a request acts on one thing): a request's object slot takes one thing, so 'the person lists a checklist' (Frame: <actor> lists <checklist>) and 'the person lists the checklists' (Frame: <actor> lists the checklists) are two requests of the one word 'list', each one reading; a plural in a request's object slot is no reading; a line that does read two ways is refused naming the words and both frames, never an empty quote. The skill guidance and the docs recipe are the chore item that b420 blocks. The person's words in quote-63.
measurement#r4 and #r8 (compare two batches, two measurements) asked one request on two objects, which decision 0111 makes unreadable; the person approved rewording them (2026-11-20), so b420 retires them with r10 and r11, read by a second compare entry of the frames '<actor> compares <batch> with <batch>' and '<actor> compares <measurement> with <measurement>' (the one-slot frames stay for measurement#r9). For the builder: the request's form is request(e, measurement.compare, subject: the person, object: x2, with: x3); the generated scenarios of r10 and r11 say one When 'the person compares the batch b1 with the batch b2' in place of the old two Whens, so telemetry_handlers.measure_compare and batch_measurement_handlers.runs_measure take both things from the one step, the object first and the 'with' slot second (the ratio is the second over the first); spec/adapters/cli.toml's measurement.compare has only 'measurement = { positional = 1 }': the 'with' slot is measure compare's second positional, which requested.py's legacy re-keying and adapter_cli's argv must reach, and its annotated requirements change with the new ids (spec annotate). The domain model's compare(measurement) is left as it is.

## Requirements

### parsing

retired: [r4]
retires: [r15 r4]

#### [g13] A requirement of a verb of two frames is read by one frame
- [r13] While a verb has two frames, when the person parses a requirement that uses a frame of the verb, the language service shall return one logical form of the requirement and complete the request.

#### [g14] A request on more than one thing is outside the language
- [r14] If the person parses a requirement that has a plural object, then the language service shall refuse the request and name the requirement.

#### [g15] A requirement of two meanings is refused naming its ambiguous text
- [r15] If the person parses a requirement that has more than one logical form, then the language service shall refuse the request and name the requirement and the ambiguous text of the requirement.

#### [g16] A requirement read by two frames of a verb is refused naming the two frames
- [r16] If the person parses a requirement whose ambiguous text uses two frames of a verb, then the language service shall refuse the request and name every frame that the ambiguous text uses.

### specification model

```model
object ambiguous text kind of text

object plural object kind of noun phrase

object requirement
  relations: has → ambiguous text | plural object (many)
```

- **ambiguous text** (noun) → object ambiguous text. The words of a requirement that two of its logical forms read two ways: two noun phrases, or the words of two frames of one verb. Plural: ambiguous texts.
- **plural object** (noun) → object plural object. A noun phrase in the plural, or with `every`, a count or a number word, after a verb that names an operation of the domain model, in a slot where a frame of the verb names a kind: a request acts on one thing. Plural: plural objects.

### measurement model

- **compare** (verb, action) → operation measurement.compare. Forms: compares, compared. Frame: <actor> compares <batch> with <batch>. Frame: <actor> compares <measurement> with <measurement>.

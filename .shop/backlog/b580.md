# Every batch summary states its cost and timing

## Notes

quote-88: what the batch's driver replies, what work batch merged prints and what a session reports of a batch include its cost (tokens and price, measure batch's figures) and its timing (opening, building, test runs, review, wall time); the brief's reply section asks the driver for them.
quote-89: cost is stated in dollars and in tokens (output, new input, cache-read).
From quote-88 and quote-89: a batch's close states what it cost (in dollars and in output, new input and cache-read tokens) and how long it took (opening, building, test runs, review, wall time): work batch merged returns it, and the batch brief asks the batch's driver to reply with it.

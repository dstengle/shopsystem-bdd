## Requirements

### batches

#### [g152] Parking an item keeps the imports another item laid on the same driven port
- [r152] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and the kind of an area is `port` and the area holds a capability that the item holds and the area imports an area whose kind is `supporting`, when the person parks another item in the batch, the batches service shall keep the area.

#### [g153] Amending an item takes out the imports of a driven port its lines no longer need
- [r153] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and a task of the item is `running` and the kind of an area is `port` and the item holds no capability that the area holds and the area imports an area whose kind is `supporting`, when the person amends the item, the batches service shall keep the area that imports no area.

### specification model

```model
object area
  relations: imports → area (many)
```

- **import** (verb, state) → relation area.imports. An area's model file names another area under its `imports:`, whose words the lines of its capabilities may say. Forms: imports, imported. Frame: <area> imports <area>.

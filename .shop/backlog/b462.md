## Requirements

### checking

#### [g59] `spec catalogue entry` writes a catalogue entry's purpose
- [r59] When the person describes a catalogue entry with a purpose, the specification service shall keep a catalogue entry that has the purpose and complete the request.

#### [g60] `spec catalogue entry` writes the item a catalogue entry came by
- [r60] When the person describes a catalogue entry with an item, the specification service shall keep a catalogue entry that mentions the item and complete the request.

#### [g61] `spec catalogue entry` refuses an entry of no rule and no construct
- [r61] If the person describes a catalogue entry that no rule has and that no construct has, then the specification service shall refuse the request and name the catalogue entry.

### batches

#### [g78] `work settings set` writes a setting in its own table
- [r78] When the person configures a setting with a setting value, the batches service shall keep a settings file that holds the setting and that conforms to the delivery settings form and complete the request.

#### [g79] `work settings set` refuses a setting the plugin does not read
- [r79] If the person configures a setting that does not conform to the delivery settings form, then the batches service shall refuse the request and name the setting.

- **the delivery settings form** (proper noun) → object delivery settings form. The settings the plugin reads in `.shop/delivery.toml`: each key under its own table and its value of its own type.

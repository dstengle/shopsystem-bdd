## Requirements

### feature-file-tools

- **faithful** (condition of feature file): a generated feature file each of whose scenarios writes every fact of its group's requirements as a step: no step holds a bracketed placeholder `[<fun>]` (a negated `exit with an error` among them); an object of a negative response keeps its modifier or relation in its Then (`does not lend a book that is out`, not `does not lend the book b1`); a `no` and an `every` in a precondition are each written as a Given; and every instance its When or a Then names, other than one a Then writes, is introduced by a Given.

#### [g20] `features generate` writes every fact of a requirement as a step
- [r20] When the person runs `features generate`, `features` shall write a feature file that is faithful.

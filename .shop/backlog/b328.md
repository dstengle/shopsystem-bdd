# 'every K in the Y, ordered by T' after a verb whose frame names K reads one way again

## Notes

Fix of batch-40's defect of b300 (the defect record under .shop/batches/40/): 'every K in the Y, ordered by T' after a verb whose frame names K reads two ways since b300 and is refused, where 0.35.1 read it one way. A line that parsed must parse to the same one meaning: either the two trees are one meaning (spec_meaning: the order of the Ks given equals the collection's order of its Ks), or one reading is preferred; b300's shaping notes asked the implementer to check exactly this. Blocks release 0.35.2 (.shop/records/2026-11-16-release-0.35.2/plan.md).

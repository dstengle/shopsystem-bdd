# The product's declarations are checked for their form and order

## Notes

New-user trial 3 of 2026-11-26 (quote-104): spec actor before any spec product is committed with no id; spec product garden (no dotted domain) accepted; a second spec product silently replaces the id; spec actor gardener (no 'the') and spec subdomain Watering_Rota accepted.
New-user trial 3 (quote-104): spec actor before any product kept an actor in a product with no id; spec product garden (no dotted domain) accepted; a second spec product replaced the id; spec actor gardener (no 'the') and spec subdomain Watering_Rota accepted. The product service refuses each: an actor before the product has an id, a product id not of the product id form (a dotted domain then a path, as example.org/notes), a product declared again with another id (declared again with its own id it keeps its details, r8), a product actor not of the product actor form (the and a noun, as the writer) and a subdomain id not of the subdomain id form (lower-case words joined by hyphens, as note-keeping), as the docs' examples give them. Restates product.declare, declare actor and declare subdomain, keeping their reasons.

## Requirements

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation declare(product, id, purpose)  outcomes: done → the product | refused: product not in the product form, id not in the product id form, id other than the product's id
  operation declare actor(product actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor | refused: product not in the product form, product with no id, product actor not in the product actor form
  operation declare subdomain(subdomain, id, subdomain type, job)  outcomes: done → the subdomain | refused: subdomain type not in the subdomain type form, job outside the product, id not in the subdomain id form

object product id form kind of artifact

object product actor form kind of artifact

object subdomain id form kind of artifact
```

- **the product id form** (proper noun) → object product id form. A dotted domain, then a path, as `example.org/notes`, the id a product is declared with.
- **the product actor form** (proper noun) → object product actor form. `the` and a noun, as `the writer`, the name a product actor is declared by.

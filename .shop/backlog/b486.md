# work batch amend is all or nothing: the base's commit undone with the branches, its errors named as amend, and the amended lines checked by the readiness bar

## Notes

batch-61 review-1 observation (b460): amend commits the renumbered item and running task on the base before laying on the task branch; a failure resets the branches but keeps the base commit. lay_lines reports a features-generate failure as 'work batch start:'. A reworded line on an in-batch item never passes the readiness bar before amend lays it.

## Requirements

### batches

#### [g89] An in-batch item amended that does not meet the readiness bar is refused and nothing changes
- [r89] While an item in a batch is `in-batch` and the item does not meet the readiness bar, if the person amends the item, then the batches service shall refuse the request, name the item and change nothing.

# An item's model lines change an existing operation: a parameter added, renamed or removed, an outcome or a reason added

## Notes

The lay of an item's model block (scripts/backlog_model.py merged/lay) only appends member lines to an element the area declares: an operation is one line of its service, its parameters, outcomes and reasons inline, so an item cannot add, rename or remove a parameter or add an outcome or a reason of an operation the model declares already; it would be appended as a second operation of the same name (v_m8). A model line that restates an operation (same service and name) is laid in place of the operation's line, and put back on unlay (park, abandon, the readiness bar's copy). From the missing-command research, the person's words in quote-68.

# 'only' keeps its meaning in the facts: U7 obliges no worker to change every file, and trials r2 keeps its verb

## Notes

Found by b112's research, phase 2 (.shop/records/2026-11-08-cnl-research-phase-2/sample-semantics.md §1.19, §3.4 fault 1): spec_facts' respond() emits only(...) and returns before resp, so trials r2 ('`trial-runner` shall read only a target') becomes 'there is a target' (verb and actor lost); in U7 ('A worker shall change only files in its worktree') unwrap() strips Only and the plural becomes every, so the facts oblige a worker to change every file in its worktree, the opposite of the rule. check.lp has no rule for only/3. Related: b114 (constructs the check does not read).

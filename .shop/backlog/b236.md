# features generate picks a verb's frame by its referents' kinds, not by its role labels alone

## Notes

Met readying b156 (2026-11-10). A verb with two frames whose roles carry the same labels, as migration-tools' supersede (Frame: ledger entry supersedes superseded entry. Frame: design-decision supersedes design-decision.), gets no step for its second frame: `spec_steps4.Reader.frame` returns the first frame whose role labels match the atom's, without looking at the kinds of the atom's referents, so `np` then refuses "no step of a design-decision in a slot of superseded entry" (b156's g28, g29). The frame to take is the one whose subject and slot kinds are ancestors of the referents' kinds (the frame the parse itself typed the clause by). Merging the frames into one, `text supersedes text`, is no way round: the parse then reads b156's r29 and r30 two ways, the relative clause on the design-decision or on the ledger entry.

Also met readying b159 (2026-11-10): decision-tiers' enforce (Frame: test enforces design-decision. Frame: requirement enforces design-decision.) gets no step for its second frame, so b159's g15 (r15, "no requirement enforces the design-decision") has no scenario: "no step of a requirement in a slot of test". With the frames swapped, g14, g16 and g17 fail the same way; nothing else of b159 fails.

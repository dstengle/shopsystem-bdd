## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **timed** (adjective of requirement): a requirement that compares a moment or a duration with "after", "before", "since" or "within the last".
- **clocked** (adjective of requirement): a requirement whose trigger is the clock reaching a moment.
- **historical** (adjective of requirement): a requirement whose While clause says that an agent has done an action, as in "the batch has published a pull request".
- **unitless** (adjective of requirement): a requirement with a number where a duration is due and no unit of the time pack after it.

#### [g71] `model parse` reads a comparison of moments
- [r71] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is timed, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g72] `model parse` reads the clock as a trigger
- [r72] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is clocked, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g73] `model parse` reads the perfect as history
- [r73] When the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is historical, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g74] `model parse` refuses a duration with no unit
- [r74] If the person runs `model parse` on a requirement that is unitless, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the requirement.

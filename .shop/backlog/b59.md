# work's bounded push ends even when a process outside its group holds its pipes

## Notes

From .shop/batches/8/review-1.md (f2, observation, b56): on a timeout shop_files.bounded kills the push's process group and then calls proc.communicate() with no timeout, which waits for EOF on the pipes; a process that started its own session and kept them open (an ssh ControlPersist master forked by the push) is not killed with the group, so the push hangs past its timeout. Give the second communicate() a short timeout too, and when it runs out close proc.stdout and proc.stderr and report the push timed out. Not reproduced; from reading the code.

# A Frame: refusal says what makes fixed words after the verb read, and recipes.md's Frame example holds in a new context

## Notes

New-user trial of 2026-11-26 (quote-103): recipes.md shows 'Frame: <actor> lists the characters of <note>.'; the same shape 'Frame: <actor> lists the chores of <member>.' gave v_t13 'does not read at "the chores of member": the verb's object is a kind or an actor of the glossary (nearest: the chores service, the member)', angle brackets dropped and unrelated suggestions; a relation member has chore did not help.
New-user trial of 2026-11-26: the Frame: shape of docs/recipes.md's 'One verb for several operations' (fixed words, then a kind: '<actor> lists the characters of <note>.') reads in grammar 4 but v_t13 refuses it (spec lexicon --check on tests/scripts/fixtures/docs-checklists raises v_t13 on both 'the characters of note' and 'the length of note'), and its message drops the angle brackets; the person's words in quote-103 and quote-104.
Rating: value 3, a new user following recipes.md hits a false v_t13 error on the documented shape; urgency 3, in the wave the person asked built first (quote-104); size 2, spec_frames.read accepts fixed words before a kind (as grammar 4 already reads) and misread names the frame as written. r151's message says the frame with its angle brackets and what fixed words after the verb may be (words of no kind before the object, or the whole object, on a verb naming an operation), dropping nearest-name suggestions that are unrelated.
Rated medium-value, high-urgency, small for the reasons above.

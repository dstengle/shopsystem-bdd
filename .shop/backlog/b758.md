### laying model

```model
service laying
  operation prepare(workspace, batch)  outcomes: done
```

- **prepare** (verb, action) → operation laying.prepare. The batches service has a workspace in which the items of a batch are laid made whole for the batch, once for all of them: the features of the capabilities they reach generated, their logical forms kept in the corpus, the catalogues and spec/index.md's Composition written. Forms: prepares, prepared. Frame: <actor> prepares <workspace> for <batch>.

### laying model

```model
service laying
  operation lay(batch, workspace)  outcomes: done
```

The batches service has the lines, model lines, terms and other pieces of every item a batch holds taken together into the specification a workspace holds, and their scenarios generated there once. Forms: lays, laid. Frame: <actor> lays <batch> in <workspace>.

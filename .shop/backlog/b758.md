# A batch's items are laid together again, and the reasons and module map rows say the laying port

## Notes

batch-139 review on b611: each picked item is now laid on its own through the laying port, so features, catalogues and the composition are written once per item (a batch of several opens slower, a catalogue problem named twice); work batch start prints every reason joined by '; ', which no line asks for; CLAUDE.md's batch_generator and batch_reached rows and batch_generator's docstring still say batch_start lays and unlays.

## Requirements

### batches

retired: [r179, r180, r130]
retires: [r185 r179, r186 r180, r187 r130]

#### [g185] The items allocated to a batch are laid together in the batch's workspace
- [r185] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item has no dependency on an item whose status is not `delivered` and the item does not exceed the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall allocate the item, keep a batch that holds the item, keep a batch plan of the batch and lay the batch in the workspace of the batch.

#### [g186] Each item of a batch the laying service has laid is checked in the workspace
- [r186] While a batch holds an item, when the laying service completes the request to lay the batch in a workspace, the batches service shall check the item in the workspace.

#### [g187] A ready item that no longer meets the readiness bar at opening is reopened naming each bar check it does not meet
- [r187] While an item is `ready` and the item does not meet the readiness bar and the item does not exceed the review limit, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall reopen the item and name every bar check that the item does not meet.

### laying

#### [g5] The items of a batch laid together are held by the workspace
- [r5] While a batch holds an item and the item holds a requirement, when the batches service lays the batch in a workspace, the laying service shall keep the workspace that holds the requirement and keep a scenario that refers to the requirement id of the requirement.
Relied on by: batches#r185

#### [g6] A check of an item that violates a rule of severity error after its batch is laid fails naming the rule
- [r6] While a requirement of an item violates a rule whose severity is `error`, when the batches service checks the item in a workspace, the laying service shall fail the request and name the rule.
Relied on by: batches#r186

#### [g7] A check of an item that violates no rule of severity error after its batch is laid completes
- [r7] While an item holds no requirement that violates a rule whose severity is `error`, when the batches service checks the item in a workspace, the laying service shall complete the request.
Relied on by: batches#r186

### laying model

```model
service laying
  operation lay(batch, workspace)  outcomes: done
```

- **lay** (verb, action) → operation laying.lay. The batches service has the lines, model lines, terms and other pieces of every item a batch holds taken together into the specification a workspace holds, and their scenarios generated there once. Forms: lays, laid. Frame: <actor> lays <batch> in <workspace>.

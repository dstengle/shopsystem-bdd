## Requirements

### applying

retired: [r1]
retires: [r37 r1]

#### [g31] An item applied whose requirements use no construct keeps no construct catalogue
- [r31] While an item holds no requirement that uses a construct and no construct catalogue exists, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep no construct catalogue.

#### [g32] An item applied keeps an index file of the index form
- [r32] When the batches service applies an item to a specification, the specification service shall keep an index file that conforms to the index form.

#### [g33] An item applied keeps a capability with no requirement out of the index file
- [r33] While a capability has no requirement, when the batches service applies an item to a specification, the specification service shall keep an index file that does not hold the capability.

#### [g34] An item applied keeps a capability with a requirement in the index file
- [r34] While a capability has a requirement, when the batches service applies an item to a specification, the specification service shall keep an index file that holds the capability.

#### [g35] An item applied generates the feature of a capability whose operation its model lines specify
- [r35] While an item holds a model line that specifies an operation and a requirement of a capability governs the operation and the item holds no requirement id that is in the capability, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall generate the feature of the capability and keep the logical form of the requirement in the corpus.

#### [g36] An item applied keeps in the corpus the logical form of a requirement that uses a verb it gives a sense
- [r36] While an item holds a verb that has a sense and a requirement of a capability uses the verb and the item holds no requirement id that is in the capability, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep the logical form of the requirement in the corpus.

#### [g37] Applying an item prepares the specification: scenarios, corpus, catalogues and index
- [r37] While an item holds a requirement, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep the specification that holds the requirement and keep a scenario that refers to the requirement id of the requirement.

#### [g38] An item applied keeps the logical form of each of its requirements in the corpus
- [r38] While a requirement of an item has a logical form, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep the logical form in the corpus.

#### [g39] An item applied keeps no corpus block of a requirement id it retires
- [r39] While a requirement id is in an item and a requirement in the corpus has the requirement id, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep a corpus that does not hold the requirement.

#### [g40] An item applied keeps the catalogue entry of a rule its requirements use
- [r40] While a requirement of an item uses a rule, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep the catalogue entry of the rule.

#### [g41] An item applied keeps the construct catalogue when its requirements use a construct
- [r41] While an item holds a requirement that uses a construct, when the batches service applies the item to a specification, the specification service shall keep the construct catalogue.

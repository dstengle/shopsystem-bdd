# The fix task step names the task the failure belongs to and asks for a commit the review accepts

## Notes

batch-120 review on b696: when the failing tests match no item's scenario and no task's change, fix task falls back to the first task; after a review is recorded, the step asks for a commit citing only Item:, which work batch review refuses (commits since a review must cite an open finding).

## Requirements

### batches

#### [g152] A batch whose submit broke a scenario of no item asks the driver to look at the failure
- [r152] While a batch is `building` and every task of the batch is `done` and a scenario breaks in the workspace of the batch and no scenario of an item in the batch breaks in the workspace of the batch, if the driver polls the batch, then the batches service shall return a batch step whose instruction is `look at failure` and the scenario.

### reviews

#### [g14] A fix after a review of a scenario its item's change broke is accepted on submit
- [r14] While a batch is `building` and every task of the batch is `done` and a review of the batch exists and an item in the batch is `in-batch` and a scenario of the item succeeds in the workspace of the batch and the workspace of a task of the item holds a kept change that cites the item, when the person submits the batch, the batches service shall keep a proposed change of the batch and set the status of the batch to `in-review`.

### execution model

```model
object batch step
  attributes: instruction: prepare task | build task | finish task | submit batch | review change | fix finding | fix task | look at failure | park item | stop
  relations: holds → task | batch brief | review folder | findings file | review finding (many)
```

- **look at failure** (noun) → value batch step.instruction look at failure. A batch step that asks the driver to look at the scenarios that broke a submit when they are of no task's item, with no task to fix named.

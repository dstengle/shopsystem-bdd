# Comparisons and orders over a value order: one thing's value compared with another's, and a collection or a printed every ordered by an attribute, in ascending or descending order

## Notes

b266 and b267 merged into one item, 2026-11-15. Each item must pass the readiness bar alone and laid over the items picked before it, and both need the order of an attribute's values in their refusal lines. Defined in both, the term value order gave v_t6 ("the kind value order is given 2 times"); defined in one, the other failed alone. So one item owns the value order, the `Type:` field and both refusals. The person's words: quote-29 ("Shape all four, fixes first, then ordering and comparisons"), quote-30, quote-31. Grounds: decision 0093 and the record .shop/records/2026-11-15-collections-review.md (known limits #6 and #14). The notes of b266 and b267, both withdrawn for this item, hold the detail of each part. (1) Value order. An attribute entry may say `Type: number.`, `Type: time.` or `Type: text.`, read by spec_lexicon and spec_value_kinds beside `Values (adjective|noun):` and `Order:`. A value of a typed attribute is any literal of that type (a number, a moment, a quoted text), not only a listed code, and a typed attribute needs no `Order:` (decision 0093 point 3). An attribute of named values keeps its `Order:` (`by number`, `as listed`). The term value order is the order either gives. A comparison or an order on a term with no value order is refused, naming the term (spec_grammar4, by spec_lf_order). (2) `value-compare` and `value-same` (b266). "the T of x is (not) after|before the T of y", both sides one attribute word of one value order, and "the T of x is (not) the same as the T of y" for any attribute. The form keeps one atom, cmp(release(x1), after, release(x2)) (spec_rgl_np's T_CmpT and T_SameAs, spec_lf_order). The check reads it as check.lp's two-place ccmp2(R,X,T,Op,Y,Pol) over the cells of the points the pair names (spec_lf_facts_atoms). Two lines whose comparisons of the same two values contradict never fire together, so their opposite acts are no L1. A cycle of comparisons through three or more things in one gap of a dense order is taken as able to hold, so an L1 may be reported that could never fire, but none is hidden. Its scenario gives each thing a value that meets every comparison and value the line names. Known limit #6 goes. migration-tools r77, r78 and r79 are retired, in the batch once the constructs parse, for lines saying "the release of this repository is the same as the release of `migrate`" (r77, r79) and "is after the release of `migrate`" (r78), with the term release (attribute of tool). r80 stays, because its 0.33.0 is the release the step upgrades from. The comparison words are after and before for every ordered type; "more than", "at least" and the others between two values are left to later. (3) `order-by` and `order-by-every` (b267). "ordered by <attribute>, in ascending order" or "in descending order", after a collection noun that holds members ("a batch that holds every item whose status is `ready`, ordered by score, in descending order") or after a printed "every K" of a response ("`migrate` shall print every upgrade step, ordered by release, in ascending order"). The attribute is one of the member kind, found up the kind tree. Modules: grammar/Base.gf and spec_rgl_np, and a new spec_lf_ordered.py that gives one translation in each place a line is read: the form's one atom ordered(x, T, ascending|descending) and its line in `model parse --form`, the check's fact, and the Givens and Then. In spec_lf_facts and check.lp the atom is a fact: two lines that require one collection, or one printing, in opposite orders on one trigger are an L1, and nothing else is proved, with no pairs. spec_steps4 writes three members with distinct values of the attribute, in neither the order nor its reverse (2, 3, 1), never a tie, and a Then that states the order. features_vocab4 reads that Then, and a shared handler in tests/scenarios checks it by a sorted comparison of the actual output, non-strict so ties pass. Docs: grammar/language.md (#14 narrowed) and model_constructs_table with grammar/constructs.md, whose example context gains "score (attribute of item): Type: number.". The existing code `ordered` is the comparison of a value with a code, which this item keeps. Left out: a then-by key, position, first and top n, uniqueness, type-flavoured words ("newest first"), and number words between two values. Depends on b268, b272 and b273.

# Grammar 4 binds a relative clause's "the K of X" to its head, and scopes "the K of every Y" wrongly

## Notes

Found shaping b156: in a response, 'write a design-decision that supersedes the design-decision of the superseded entry' reads as supersede(x, x) with x of the superseded entry (model parse --form), and 'print the total row of every measurement' as one total row of every measurement (the of atom inside the every box, the K outside it). b156 wrote around both (a design-decision of the ledger entry that supersedes a design-decision of the superseded entry; the K of the trigger's referent). Bind a definite inside a relative clause to an earlier referent, never to its head, and put 'the K of every Y' inside the every box.
Shaped 2026-11-10 for the rewords b152-b159 (b156's migration-tools r29 and batch-measurement's total row met it). Reproduced on main with model parse --form: 'When the person runs `work batch review` on a batch, `work` shall write a review that lists the review of the pull request.' gives list(subject: x5, object: x5) with of(x5, x6): R2 binds 'the review' inside the relative clause to the head it modifies; and 'When the person runs `work batch status`, `work` shall print the pull request of every batch.' puts the pull request outside the every box (one pull request of every batch, also of the trigger's batch). Lines in design-model-check and feature-file-tools (codified-1, so each construct is a term, and each reading a noun kind of logical form): 'the K of X' in a relative clause on a K is the image of K of X, a referent of its own; 'the K of every Y' is inside the every box, one K of each Y; features generate writes both. Depends on b217: the same R2 rule in spec_lf_read (pass over the noun phrase that holds the definite), which the first reading extends to a relative clause.

## Requirements

### design-model-check

- **self-relating** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, with "the K of X" inside a relative clause on a noun phrase of kind K, as in "When the person runs `work batch review` on a batch, `work` shall write a review that lists the review of the pull request.".
- **relation reading** (noun): a logical form in which "the K of X" inside a relative clause on a noun phrase of kind K is a referent of its own, the K of X, and not the referent of that noun phrase. Plural: relation readings. Kind of: logical form.
- **distributive** (adjective of requirement): a requirement of a capability whose file has `language: codified-2`, with "the K of every Y" in its response, as in "When the person runs `work batch status`, `work` shall print the pull request of every batch.".
- **distributed reading** (noun): a logical form in which "the K of every Y" is a referent inside the box of "every Y", one K of each Y, and not one K of all of them. Plural: distributed readings. Kind of: logical form.

#### [g160] `model parse --form` reads "the K of X" in a relative clause on a K as its own referent
- [r160] When the person runs `model parse --form` on a requirement that is self-relating, `model` shall print a relation reading and exit successfully.

#### [g161] `model parse --form` reads "the K of every Y" inside the box of every Y
- [r161] When the person runs `model parse --form` on a requirement that is distributive, `model` shall print a distributed reading and exit successfully.

# Grammar 4 binds a relative clause's "the K of X" to its head, and scopes "the K of every Y" wrongly

## Notes

Found shaping b156: in a response, 'write a design-decision that supersedes the design-decision of the superseded entry' reads as supersede(x, x) with x of the superseded entry (model parse --form), and 'print the total row of every measurement' as one total row of every measurement (the of atom inside the every box, the K outside it). b156 wrote around both (a design-decision of the ledger entry that supersedes a design-decision of the superseded entry; the K of the trigger's referent). Bind a definite inside a relative clause to an earlier referent, never to its head, and put 'the K of every Y' inside the every box.

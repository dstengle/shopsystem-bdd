# work line --remove keeps a new capability's declaration

## Notes

From the missing-commands and new-user-flow research of 2026-11-21: `backlog_reword.py:61` deletes the block when it has no groups, terms, relations, retired ids or language, but never checks `block.new` (narrator, group, purpose). Removing the last line of a capability new on the item drops the whole `### <cap>` block, and the next `--into` is refused (reproduced in `commands-skeptic/ctx/`). Count `block.new` as content, or refuse and name the command that drops the capability. The person's words in quote-68.

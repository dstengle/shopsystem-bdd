# A verb agrees with its subject's number: 'When sessions write a file' parses and 'When sessions writes a file' is refused

## Notes

Found by b112's research, phase 1 (.shop/records/2026-11-08-cnl-research-phase-1/gf-cnls.md §3.2): a verb frame's subject slot <T>_Sb has lincat Str and e__Happens always uses the third person singular, so 'When sessions writes a file, ...' parses (k_session_Pl) and 'When sessions write a file, ...' is refused with 'the verb write takes as its subject agent or a kind of it', naming the wrong cause. A fix stands whatever b112 decides about the RGL.

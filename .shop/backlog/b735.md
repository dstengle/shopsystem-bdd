# Keeping an obtained contract in its provider's adapter spec is a contract registry operation

## Notes

batch-131 review on b636: contract_obtain imports the folder adapter's place and writes provider/<id>/<version>/ itself, against decision 0139/0147 (locations are the adapter's). Give the registry an operation for keeping an obtained contract for an adapter spec; delivering it supersedes 0137 at requirement tier.
batch-131's review of b636 found contract_obtain importing the shop-folder adapter's place and writing provider/<id>/<version>/ itself, against decision 0147 (locations are the adapter's; 0139 before it). The contract registry gets an operation, register, that keeps an obtained contract for an adapter spec; spec obtain requests it for each adapter spec of the provider and acts on its reply (decisions 0147, 0137, 0139, 0125; b636, b626, b724).

## Requirements

### checking

retired: [r91, r100]
retires: [r132 r91, r133 r100]

#### [g132] An obtained contract is registered with the contract registry for each adapter spec of its provider
- [r132] While an adapter spec has a provider, when the person obtains a contract from the provider, the specification service shall register the contract for the adapter spec with the provider.

#### [g133] An obtained contract is registered at the version its adapter spec pins, not a later one
- [r133] While an adapter spec has a provider and the provider has the `1.2.0` version and the provider has a contract that has the `1.3.0` version, when the person obtains a contract from the provider, the specification service shall register the contract for the adapter spec with the provider and the `1.2.0` version.

#### [g134] A registration the contract registry completes keeps the obtained contract
- [r134] When the contract registry service completes the request to register a contract for an adapter spec, the specification service shall keep the contract.

#### [g135] A registration the contract registry refuses names the adapter spec and changes nothing
- [r135] When the contract registry service refuses the request to register a contract for an adapter spec, the specification service shall name the adapter spec and change nothing.

### contract-registry

#### [g4] A contract registered for an adapter spec is kept by the registry
- [r4] When the specification service registers a contract for an adapter spec, the contract registry service shall complete the request.
Relied on by: checking#r132, checking#r133

### registry model

```model
service contract registry
  operation register(contract, adapter spec, provider, version)  outcomes: done → the contract | refused: contract not registered
```

- **register** (verb, action) → operation contract registry.register. The specification service gives the contract registry a contract obtained from a provider to keep for an adapter spec of the provider, at the version the adapter spec reads. Forms: registers, registered. Frame: <actor> registers <contract> for <adapter spec>.

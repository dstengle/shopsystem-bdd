# A capability new on an item is placed in the area the person names

## Notes

Found shaping the product layer (b602): work line --capability writes no area: of its own, so a new capability's area is its own name and a new model file importing nothing, which cannot read the specification area's job or bounded context. Technical call: work line <b> --capability <name> --area <area> (an area of spec/model/ or new), the block's area: line, laid as the capability's area: and its row of the composition table; without --area the capability's own name as today.

## Requirements

### drafting

#### [g66] A new capability drafted with an area is kept in that area
- [r66] When the person drafts an item with a capability and an area, the drafting service shall keep the capability in the item and keep the area that holds the capability in the item.

### batches

#### [g116] A new capability laid with its area is kept in that area
- [r116] While an item is `ready` and the item meets the readiness bar and the item holds a capability and the item holds an area that holds the capability, when the person opens a batch, the batches service shall keep the capability that the area holds.

### tracking model

```model
service drafting
  operation draft(item, relation row, universal condition, universal condition id, upstream entry, given block, requirement id, requirement, capability, area)
    outcomes: done → the item | refused: relation row not in form, upstream entry not in form, given block not example data, requirement id of no requirement, universal condition id of no condition, chore in the item, capability that exists

object item
  relations: holds → area (many)
```

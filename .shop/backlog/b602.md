# A compact shop keeps the product's identity, actors and jobs to be done

## Notes

quote-90: with a shop wider than a bounded context (decision 0129), a compact shop includes the product layer: at minimum its jobs to be done and its domain map (subdomains, decision 0128) and which hosted contexts address them; design under discussion.
quote-91: every job to be done has one bounded context and capability that is its entry point and coordinator; the job is tied to requirement lines through it. Concrete shape open.
quote-92: not 'claims': the product assigns a job to a bounded context (the product's decision, in its context map); the capability of that context that coordinates it serves the job (capability frontmatter serves: <job>); a worked example on this repository follows.

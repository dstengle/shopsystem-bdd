# A compact shop keeps the product's identity, actors and jobs to be done

## Notes

quote-90: with a shop wider than a bounded context (decision 0129), a compact shop includes the product layer: at minimum its jobs to be done and its domain map (subdomains, decision 0128) and which hosted contexts address them; design under discussion.
quote-91: every job to be done has one bounded context and capability that is its entry point and coordinator; the job is tied to requirement lines through it. Concrete shape open.
quote-92: not 'claims': the product assigns a job to a bounded context (the product's decision, in its context map); the capability of that context that coordinates it serves the job (capability frontmatter serves: <job>); a worked example on this repository follows.
Decisions 0128, 0129, 0141, 0142. Technical calls: a new capability product in the specification area (service product, the job object already there); operations declare (the product: product id, purpose), add an actor of the product, add a job (title, actor or actors, statement, needs); a job statement is a structured entry checked for its form only (When <situation>, <actor> wants <motivation>, so they can <outcome>: the job statement form, an artifact), an actor of a job one of the product's, a needed job one the product holds and needs closing no cycle (0142). CLI commands under spec (spec product, spec actor, spec job); where the product layer is kept (product/ of the shop) is an adapter decision. Split from the product layer of a compact shop: the domain map is b642, serves: b641, the product check b643, the trace b644.
Rated: high value (the product layer's base: jobs are what every later product check and trace reads), medium urgency (the disentangling waits on it), medium size (a new capability, about six lines, the product's forms and their writers: about 600-800 changed lines). Its lines are written once b645 lets the new capability product sit in the specification area, where the job object is.

## Requirements

### product

narrator: the person
group: service
purpose: The product a bounded context belongs to: its id and purpose, its actors and the jobs to be done it holds.
area: specification

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation declare(product, id, purpose)  outcomes: done → the product
  operation declare actor(product actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor
  operation declare job(job, id, product actor, job statement, need)
    outcomes: done → the job | refused: job statement not in the job statement form, product actor outside the product, need outside the product, need that depends on the job

object product
  relations: has → id (one); has → purpose (one); holds → product actor (many); holds → job (many)

object product actor kind of text

object job statement kind of text

object job statement form kind of artifact

object need kind of job

object job
  relations: has → id (one); has → job statement (one); has → product actor (many); needs → job (many); depends on → job (many)
```

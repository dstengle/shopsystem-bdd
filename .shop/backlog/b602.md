## Requirements

### product

narrator: the person
group: service
purpose: The product a bounded context belongs to: its id and purpose, its actors and the jobs to be done it holds.
area: specification

#### [g1] The product is declared with its id and purpose
- [r1] When the person declares a product with an id and a purpose, the product service shall keep the product and complete the request.

#### [g2] An actor of the product is declared
- [r2] When the person declares a product actor, the product service shall keep the product actor in the product and complete the request.

#### [g3] A job to be done is declared with its actors and statement
- [r3] When the person declares a job with an id and a product actor and a job statement, the product service shall keep the job in the product and complete the request.

#### [g4] A job whose statement is not of the job statement form is refused
- [r4] If the person declares a job with a job statement that does not conform to the job statement form, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the job statement.

#### [g5] A job whose actor is not the product's is refused
- [r5] If the person declares a job with a product actor that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the product actor.

#### [g6] A job that needs a job the product does not hold is refused
- [r6] If the person declares a job with a need that is not in the product, then the product service shall refuse the request and name the need.

### specification model

```model
service product
  operation declare(product, id, purpose)  outcomes: done → the product
  operation declare actor(product actor)  outcomes: done → the product actor
  operation declare job(job, id, product actor, job statement, need)
    outcomes: done → the job | refused: job statement not in the job statement form, product actor outside the product, need outside the product, need that depends on the job

object product
  relations: has → id (one); has → purpose (one); holds → product actor (many); holds → job (many)

object product actor kind of text

object job statement kind of text

object job statement form kind of artifact

object need kind of job

object job
  relations: has → id (one); has → job statement (one); has → product actor (many); needs → job (many); depends on → job (many)
```

- **the product service** (proper noun) → service product.
- **product** (noun) → object product. What the bounded contexts of one product id are built for, with its purpose, its actors and its jobs to be done. Plural: products. This: the product this bounded context belongs to.
- **product actor** (noun) → object product actor. One who is served by the product, as `the developer`, whom each bounded context calls by a term of its own. Plural: product actors.
- **job statement** (noun) → object job statement. What a job is for, as one sentence of the job statement form. Plural: job statements.
- **the job statement form** (proper noun) → object job statement form. `When <situation>, <actor> wants <motivation>, so they can <outcome>.`, checked for its form only.
- **need** (noun) → object need. A job that a job needs done first, named by its id. Plural: needs.
- **declare** (verb, action) → operation product.declare. The person says the id and the purpose of the product. Forms: declares, declared. Frame: <actor> declares <product>.
- **declare** (verb, action) → operation product.declare actor. The person names one who the product serves. Forms: declares, declared. Frame: <actor> declares <product actor>.
- **declare** (verb, action) → operation product.declare job. The person writes a job to be done of the product with its id, its actors, its job statement and the jobs it needs. Forms: declares, declared. Frame: <actor> declares <job>.
- **need** (verb, state) → relation job.needs. A job is carried through another job first. Forms: needs, needed. Frame: <job> needs <job>.
- **depend on** (verb, state) → relation job.depends on. A job needs another job, directly or through the jobs it needs. Forms: depends on, depended on. Frame: <job> depends on <job>.

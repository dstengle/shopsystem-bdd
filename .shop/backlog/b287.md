# value-compare and value-same over two entries of one attribute word: the release of a repository compared with the release of a tool

## Notes

Fixes batch-35 defect-1 (.shop/batches/35/defect-1.md) of b275: spec_rgl_np builds T_CmpT and T_SameAs from one attribute entry, so 'the release of this repository is after the release of `migrate`' does not parse, though b275's note and grammar/language.md say two entries of one word with one value order compare. b284 (migration-tools r77-r79 reworded) waits on it.

## Requirements

### design-model-check

| value order | of | sense | one |

#### [g306] `model parse` reads a comparison of two senses of one term with one value order
- [r306] While a sense of a term has a value order and another sense of the term has the value order and a requirement uses the term and `value-compare`, when the person runs `model parse` on the requirement, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g307] `model parse` reads two senses of one term with one value order said to be the same
- [r307] While a sense of a term has a value order and another sense of the term has the value order and a requirement uses the term and `value-same`, when the person runs `model parse` on the requirement, `model` shall exit successfully.

#### [g308] `model parse` refuses a comparison of two senses of one term with different value orders
- [r308] While a sense of a term has a value order and another sense of the term has a different value order and a requirement uses the term and `value-compare`, if the person runs `model parse` on the requirement, then `model` shall refuse the command and name the term.

#### [g309] `model parse` refuses two senses of one term with different value orders said to be the same
- [r309] While a sense of a term has a value order and another sense of the term has a different value order and a requirement uses the term and `value-same`, if the person runs `model parse` on the requirement, then `model` shall refuse the command and name the term.

#### [g310] `model logic` finds the clash of two lines under a comparison of two senses of one term
- [r310] While a sense of a term has a value order and another sense of the term has the value order and a requirement of a capability uses the term and `value-compare` and the requirement obliges an act and another requirement of the capability that has the trigger of the requirement forbids the act, when the person runs `model logic`, `model` shall print `L1`.

## Requirements

### specification model

- **preview** (verb, action) → operation language.preview. A person parses a requirement as it reads once the terms and model lines an item holds are applied to the specification, as `spec parse --item` does. Forms: previews, previewed. Frame: <actor> previews <text> for <item>.

### tracking model

- **model line** (noun) → object model line. A line of the domain model's syntax, an element with its members or a relation, that an item keeps for the area of one of its capabilities until its batch applies it to `spec/model/<area>.md`. Plural: model lines.
- **readiness bar** (noun) → object readiness bar. The checks `work ready` makes of an item on a copy of `spec/` with the item applied to it: its request lines settled, its lines parsed, no L0 and no L1 of `model logic` naming them, their scenarios written by `features generate`, and the `check` design-decisions its capabilities rest on passing. This: the readiness bar of the plugin that runs the command.

### execution model

- **amend** (verb, action) → operation batches.amend. An item's lines applied again, after they changed, to the specification of the workspace of its task and of the task of each item that depends on it. Forms: amends, amended. Frame: <actor> amends <item>.
- **amendment** (noun) → object amendment. A kept change that work batch amend makes on the workspace of a task, applying an amended item's requirements again. Plural: amendments.

### measurement model

- **opening time** (noun) → object opening time. The time `work` takes to open a batch: its readiness check of the items again, its application of each item to its task's workspace and its run of their scenarios.
- **opening phase** (noun) → object opening phase. One part of the time `work` takes to open a batch: the pick of its items with their readiness checks, the compile of grammar 4, the parse of the lines, the apply, the features and corpus written again, the catalogues and the Composition, the check of each item, the run of the applied scenarios, or the worktree and the commit.

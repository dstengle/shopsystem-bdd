(a test can check it); (b) retire r14 with no replacement; (c) keep r14 unchanged.
2. supervision r186 vs r149: r186 (q21 line 13) is word for word r149. Readings: (a, recommended) retire r149, keeping r186, so that a work-item is drafted for the behaviour i39 found broken (frontmatter `retired` gains r149); (b) drop r186 and keep r149 alone, the bug to be fixed some other way; (c) keep both lines. r152 and r153 (same trigger: keep the status, take it as the round's output) do not conflict with r186 and stay.

Readings: (a, recommended) retire r32, which r184 covers, and retire r9 for "[r187] **When** no node of the initiative can make a transition, every work-item is `green` or `withdrawn`, no capability-proposal is `awaiting-answer` and no request whose asker is this context is in the asker's turn, `work run` shall exit successfully, printing each work-item's status."; (b) keep r32 and r9 as they are, r184 taking precedence; (c) keep r32 and r184 and retire r9 only. r81 (no initiative, any node `awaiting-answer` or `failed` → error with each initiative's status) does not conflict but overlaps in how it prints: keep (recommended) or also print each proposal's open questions under r184.

Also r31 (every work-item `withdrawn` → status left as it is) does not conflict.

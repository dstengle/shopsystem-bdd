## Questions
- supervision r98 (added), what "the reviewer's findings" are and how they are given. The line as drafted: [r98] **When** `work run` starts an implementer for a work-item that an answer to a reviewer's question returned to `ready`, `work run` shall give the implementer the reviewer's findings and the answer. Readings: (a) RECOMMENDED: the brief names the findings file; Implementation line "r98: the brief names the findings file of the work-item's last review stage directory and writes the answer as r57 does." (b) the findings' text is written into the brief; r98 reworded "... `work run` shall write the reviewer's findings and the answer in the implementer's brief."
- supervision r98, whose reviewer: the line as drafted covers any reviewer. Readings: (a) RECOMMENDED: only a work-item's reviewer (a branch review's findings get a new work-item under r74), so r98 reads "**When** `work run` starts an implementer for a work-item that an answer to the question of a work-item's reviewer returned to `ready`, `work run` shall give the implementer the reviewer's findings and the answer." (b) any reviewer, as drafted.

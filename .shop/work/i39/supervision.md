Readings: (a) add "[r137] **Where** the request an initiative fulfils gives no delegation, **when** a round of the kind `integrate` gives the lines of a request into an upstream, `work run` shall raise them with no delegation." (recommended); (b) add a line that `work run` raises nothing and writes a question for the person under the initiative's ledger.

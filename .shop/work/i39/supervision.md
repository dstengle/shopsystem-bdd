# supervision

The drafts of this proposal are in .shop/work/i39/runs/i39/1-integrate/drafts/round-1/.

## Questions
- Delegation when the request the initiative fulfils gives none. Concerns line [r135] (new): "**When** a round of the kind `integrate` that `work run` started gives the lines of a request into an upstream, `work run` shall raise them with `work request`, raised for the request the initiative fulfils and with that request's delegation." Readings: (a) add "[r137] **Where** the request an initiative fulfils gives no delegation, **when** a round of the kind `integrate` gives the lines of a request into an upstream, `work run` shall raise them with no delegation." (recommended); (b) add a line that `work run` raises nothing and writes a question for the person under the initiative's ledger. Until you choose, r135 and the new line [r136] ("**While** a request that an initiative raised into an upstream is not `delivered`, `work run` shall draft no work-item for the capability-proposal whose lines it raised.") stand with no line for the no-delegation case.
- supervision#r136: "While a request that an initiative raised into an upstream is not `delivered`, `work run` shall draft no work-item for the capability-proposal whose lines it raised." With more than one capability-proposal, which are held? Reading 1: every proposal the initiative's integrate round wrote. Reading 2: only the proposal the raised lines belong to (needs r135 to tie raised lines to a proposal).

## Answers
- 2026-10-27: r135: a delegation always passes upstream as a flag; the upstream decides what to do with that fact ('I did this under delegation' is not 'we are all supposed to do this under delegation'). Whether a shop repo acts on a delegation is a setting of that repo, on to start with.
- 2026-10-27: r136: Reading 2, only the proposal the raised lines belong to, as q14 line 25 says ('the capability-proposal whose lines it raised'); r135 ties the raised lines to the proposal whose integrate round gave them (answered by the coordinator under the person's delegation of 2026-10-27; no reading was recommended).

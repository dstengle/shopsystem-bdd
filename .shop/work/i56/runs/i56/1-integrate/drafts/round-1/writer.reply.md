## Questions

1. Glossary, term/request. The entry defines a request as "lines in strict EARS that its asker ... proposes"; q27 lets a request have a reason and no lines. (a) Change it to: `- **request**: the one way work enters a bounded context: lines in strict EARS, or a reason its lines are still to be drafted from, that its asker (another context, the product level, or the context itself) proposes to its upstream (the context asked), negotiated in turns until each is accepted, refused or withdrawn, and resolved by the requirement ids that hold the accepted ones.` (b) Leave it as it is.
2. Glossary, term/reason. "reason" is a new word of the request's form. (a) Add `- **reason**: the asker's words, given with a request in place of its lines, saying what the request is for, from which its lines are drafted.` (b) No term.
3. requests r49 and r44. Does a lineless request get what r44 gives a request with lines? (a) Yes: r49 becomes `**When** `work request <title> --from <asker>` is run with a reason and no lines, `work` shall write an `open` request with its reason and no lines, the asker given as its asker, the origins or job-thread given, and the turn the upstream's, and print its id.` (b) No, r49 as drafted, the rest left to r44 alone.
4. requests, a reason beside lines. (a) A reason is only in place of lines: add `**If** `work request` is given both a reason and lines, **then** `work request` shall refuse, because a reason is given only in place of lines, and change nothing.` (b) A reason may also come with lines: add `**Where** a reason is given to `work request`, `work` shall write the reason in the request.`
5. requests, neither. If `work request` gets neither lines nor a reason: (a) add `**If** `work request` is given neither lines nor a reason, **then** `work request` shall refuse, because the request has neither lines nor a reason, and change nothing.` (b) No line.
6. requests r9 and a lineless request. When the upstream ends its turn on a request with no lines, "every line has the line outcome refused or withdrawn" holds vacuously, so r9 would set it `refused`. (a) Change r9 (same id) to `**When** the upstream ends its turn on a request that is not `delivered` that has at least one line and whose every line has the line outcome refused or withdrawn, `work` shall set the request's status to `refused`.` (b) Leave r9; the intake round (r67) never ends the upstream's turn on a lineless request (see 9).
7. requests r4/r34, who writes the drafted lines. The lines are the asker's part, written only by `work request` or the asker's answer. (a) The asker's answer to the drafting question writes them: add `**When** the asker answers the question of a request's drafted lines accepting them, `work` shall write the drafted lines as the request's lines.` (b) The asker writes them with a further command (a new line naming it). (c) Only a request whose asker is this context may have no lines: add `**If** a request whose asker is not the upstream itself is given no lines, **then** `work request` shall refuse, because only an internal request may be raised with a reason alone, and change nothing.`
8. supervision r183, "takes in". (a) The take-in of requests r39/r40, of what is sent from another repository, as drafted. (b) Any request of which this context is the upstream, an internal one included: r183 becomes `**When** a request of which this context is the upstream is `open` or `awaiting-upstream` and has a reason and no lines, `work run` shall start a round that drafts its lines from its reason and gives them to the person as a question.`
9. supervision r183, the round and r67. (a) The round is a new kind (an `initiative-round` session of a kind named in Implementation), and add `**While** a request has no lines, `work run` shall start no round of the kind `intake` for it.` (b) It is the `intake` round, given the reason; r132 then changes to give, for an intake round of a lineless request, its drafted lines as its output.
10. supervision r183, where the question goes. (a) In the request, as the upstream's question (r100, requests r20), with the turn passed to the asker: add `**When** the round r183 starts gives a request's drafted lines, `work run` shall write them in the request as a question.` (b) To the person at `work run`'s end: add `**When** `work run` ends while a request's drafted lines have no answer, `work run` shall exit with an error, printing the request with its drafted lines.` (design-decision/exits-are-successful-or-errors: a stop to wait on the person exits with an error.)
11. work-queue r46 with r22 and supervision r103. A lineless request can never be `accepted` (requests r8 needs one accepted line), so r22 and r103 never fire for it and r46 is a guard. (a) r46 as drafted. (b) Also refuse it when run: add `**If** `work start` is run on a request that has no lines, **then** `work start` shall refuse, because the request has no lines, and change nothing.` (c) Place the guard in supervision instead: `**While** a request has no lines, `work run` shall start no initiative for it with `work start`.`
12. requests Implementation. How the reason is given and kept is not given. If wanted: `- r49: the reason is given as `--reason <text>` and kept under the request's `## Reason`.` (a) Yes, with the flag and section the person names. (b) No line; `work`'s own.
13. supervision Purpose. It lists the rounds `work run` starts. (a) Add after the `intake` round: "starts the round that drafts the lines of a request that has a reason and no lines". (b) Leave it.

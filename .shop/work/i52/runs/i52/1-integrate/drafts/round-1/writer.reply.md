## Questions

1. work-queue, r23 and r40: should other `work` commands than `work withdraw` (for example `work answer` taking a work-item to `done`, a request's `work resolve`, or a command that delivers a request) also leave `done` to `work run`? (a) Only `work withdraw`, as drafted: r23 as changed and r40 as added. (b) Every `work` command: r23 removed and its id retired, and r40 changed to "**When** a `work` command's change leaves every work-item of an initiative `green` or `withdrawn`, `work` shall leave the initiative's status as it is, for `work run` to set once the initiative's kept branch review lists nothing to fix now." Recommended: (b), since the fault (an initiative done before its branch review) is not specific to `work withdraw`. But the request names only `work withdraw`.
2. work-queue, r41: should the line hold the time and who set the status, as r5's does for a work-item? (a) Yes: "...with the time, the initiative, its status before and after, and who set it." (b) Only what the request gives, as drafted: the initiative and its status before and after.
3. work-queue, r41: does `work start` writing a `proposed` initiative count as setting its status, so that it is logged? (a) Yes: "**When** `work start` writes an initiative, `work` shall append a line to `.shop/work/<initiative>/log.jsonl` with the initiative and its status `proposed`." as a further line. (b) No: r41 covers only a change of an existing initiative's status, as drafted.
4. supervision: should `work run`'s own settings of an initiative's status (r84 `started`, r105 and r161 `done`) also be logged in the initiative's `log.jsonl`? The request names only `work`. (a) Yes, a new line r183: "**When** `work run` sets an initiative's status, `work run` shall append a line to `.shop/work/<initiative>/log.jsonl` with the initiative and its status before and after the transition." (b) No: only `work`'s, as drafted. Implementation may already route r84 and r105 through `work`, which the writer cannot see.
5. work-queue, r9: should the `log.jsonl` line r41 writes be committed with the initiative's file? (a) Yes, a change to r9 (or a new line r42): "**When** `work` sets an initiative's status, `work` shall commit on the current branch the initiative's file and the `log.jsonl` line it wrote." (b) No line: left as it is.
6. supervision, r181: which glossary-proposal statuses does it apply to? Keeping the status of an `approved` proposal would let added terms skip the gate. (a) Any status, as drafted. (b) Only a proposal that is `drafted` or `awaiting-answer`: r181 gets "and the initiative has a glossary-proposal that is neither `approved` nor `withdrawn`", and a further line says what happens otherwise. For example: "**When** ... the initiative's glossary-proposal is `approved`, `work run` shall add the terms to it and set it `drafted`, keeping its questions and answers." Recommended: (b).
7. supervision, r181: if a term the round gives is already in the glossary-proposal (added earlier, now changed again), should it replace the earlier entry or be listed twice? (a) Replace: "...shall add each term ..., a term the proposal already lists replacing its entry, keeping ...". (b) Silent, as drafted.
8. supervision, r182: "until the person has answered" can be read two ways. (a) Until the person has answered that question, as drafted: sessions under the delegation start again once no question left for the person is unanswered. (b) Until the person has answered every question of the initiative: "**While** a question of the initiative has no answer and one of its questions is left for the person, ...".
9. supervision, r182 and r163: once the person has answered, does `work run` give the initiative's remaining questions to sessions under the delegation again? (a) Yes, r163 resumes, with no new line. (b) No, every remaining question is left for the person: "**When** a question of an initiative has been left for the person, `work run` shall leave every later question of that initiative for the person."
10. supervision, r182: does a question left for the person in an earlier delivery hold back sessions in a later `work run`? (a) Yes: it is a state on the initiative, as drafted. (b) No: only within one delivery, giving "**While** a delivery has left a question of an initiative for the person and ...".

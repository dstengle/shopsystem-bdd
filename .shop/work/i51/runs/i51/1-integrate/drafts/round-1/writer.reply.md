## Questions

1. supervision r174/r175: "mentions the glossary" can be read two ways. (a) The question's text names the glossary itself, which r176 makes every question about a term do. Under this reading the lines stand as drafted. (b) The question is about the glossary: it names a term of the glossary, a glossary-proposal or the glossary. Under this reading r174 would be "**When** a question of an initiative names the glossary, a term of the glossary or a glossary-proposal, `work run` shall leave the question for the person, giving it to no session under a delegation.", and r175 would say "that names neither the glossary, a term of the glossary nor a glossary-proposal". Which reading is meant?
2. supervision r176: does "a round or a worker" cover only rounds and workers that `work run` starts? The glossary's worker is a session for one stage of one work-item. Or does it also cover the branch reviewer and the answering sessions under a delegation, which the glossary does not count as workers? If they are included, the line would be: "**When** a session that `work run` started writes a question on the definition of a term or on a proposed term, the session shall name the glossary in the question."

## Questions

None. I read "the drafts it applied do not hold" as "the applied drafts have no line for the answer". The scenario uses an answer that no applied line covers at all. That case fits this reading and also the narrower reading where "do not hold" means "contradict". So the scenario does not choose between them.

## Cases no line mentions

- A round of the kind apply where the applied drafts hold every answer: the line doesn't say whether the result says so outright or just gives nothing.
- A round of the kind apply that ends by stopping (r33) before applying any drafts: the line doesn't say whether every answer is then given as unheld.
- An answer that the applied drafts hold only in part: the line doesn't say whether it counts as held.
- An answer that asks for no line at all, such as one that declines a line: the line doesn't say whether it can be "not held".

## Questions
- supervision r45 (a worker's session ending in an error, at its turn limit or with no JSON result). Reading A (drafted, recommended): r45 stays as the work-item's way into `failed` for that fault, only 'whatever its result says' dropped, since r128 says it for every session: '- [r45] **If** a worker's session ends in an error or at its turn limit, exits with an error, or leaves output that holds no JSON result, then `work run` shall transition the work-item to `failed`, recording how the session ended.' (was: the same, ending '..., recording how the session ended, whatever its result says.') Reading B: r45 is wholly replaced by r128 with r42 and the `failed` glossary entry; r45 is removed and its id retired.
- supervision r4 (the implementer's merge). Was: '**When** an implementer's outcome is `DONE`, `work run` shall merge its work-item branch into the initiative branch only if ...'. Reading A (drafted, recommended): merge only when the result gives no question (r130's condition): '- [r4] **When** an implementer's session ends with a result that gives no question, `work run` shall merge its work-item branch into the initiative branch only if the work-item's scenarios pass on it and the whole suite passes on it but for the failing scenarios r43 counts as expected.' Reading B: merge whatever questions the result gives, the checks alone deciding: '- [r4] **When** an implementer's session ends and no fault stopped its stage, `work run` shall merge its work-item branch into the initiative branch only if the work-item's scenarios pass on it and the whole suite passes on it but for the failing scenarios r43 counts as expected.' (B conflicts with r26, which keeps the branch of a work-item sent to `awaiting-answer`.)
- supervision r80, r71, r114 (apply round) and r83 (formulate round) say 'ends' with no condition on the output, while r131 (new: '**When** a session that `work run` started ends with a result that gives neither a question nor its stage's output, `work run` shall write under the node's `## Questions` a question naming the output missing and transition the node to `awaiting-answer`.') sends a round that gives no question and no output to `awaiting-answer`. Reading A (recommended): they stay as written, r130/r131 deciding whether the next transition happens and these lines naming it. Reading B: each is narrowed to the output, e.g. '- [r80] **When** a round of the kind `apply` that `work run` started for a capability-proposal ends with a result that gives no question and its change list, `work run` shall transition the proposal to `approved`.' (likewise r71 and r114), and '- [r83] **When** a round of the kind `formulate` that `work run` started for a capability-proposal ends with a result that gives no question and a scenario for each requirement its proposal adds or changes, `work run` shall transition the proposal to `formulated`.'
- supervision r117, r69, r85 (gate round) against r131 and r132 (a gate round's output is its questions). When a gate round gives no question: Reading A (recommended): r117 holds (one question asking for approval of the lines as drafted, then r69's `awaiting-answer`), and r131 does not apply to a gate round; no line changes. Reading B: r131 applies (a question naming the missing questions) and r117 is removed and retired. Separately, r85 says what r129 says for a gate round: Reading A (recommended): keep r85. Reading B: remove r85 as covered by r129 (and drop 'r85' from the Implementation line 'r68, r85: ...').
- supervision r129 ('**When** a session that `work run` started ends with a result that gives questions, `work run` shall send each question where its stage's questions go: ... transitioning the node to `awaiting-answer`.') and r131 for an intake round, whose node is a request, and a request has no status `awaiting-answer`. Reading A (recommended): for an intake round the question goes in the request (r100) and the request's turn passes with `work turn` (r67), with no `awaiting-answer`; a line is added: '- [r134] **When** a round of the kind `intake` that `work run` started gives a question, `work run` shall leave the request's status as `work turn` writes it.' Reading B: r129 and r131 apply as written and the request's lifecycle (requests capability, out of this scope) gains `awaiting-answer`.
- supervision r96 (an integrate round ending without a change list exits with an error) against r131 (a question and `awaiting-answer`; an initiative has no status `awaiting-answer`). Reading A (recommended): r96 stays, an initiative not being a node r131 transitions. Reading B: r96 is removed and retired, r131 writing the question under the initiative's `## Questions`, and the initiative lifecycle gaining `awaiting-answer` (outside this request).
- supervision r77 and r131 for an answering session whose delegation does not cover the question (its output, the answer, missing, no question given). Reading A (recommended): r131 does not apply; the question is left for the person as r77 says, no line changes. Reading B: r131 applies, writing a question naming the missing answer under the node's `## Questions`; r77's 'leaves it for the person otherwise' would be removed by a change.
- supervision r44 (the brief tells the worker to write its result first with the outcome `PARTIAL`). Reading A (drafted, recommended): kept, as it decides no transition. Reading B: removed and retired, since after r133 ('`work run` shall make no transition from the outcome a session's result gives.') the outcome decides nothing; 'continuations after `PARTIAL`' stays in Not yet either way (the delegation says it leaves Not yet; the draft left it unchanged, so whether to remove it is open).
- Glossary, term/fault: r128 ('**If** a session that `work run` started for a work-item, a capability-proposal, an initiative or a question ends in an error or at its turn limit, or leaves no result `work run` can read, **then** `work run` shall take the session's end as a fault, whatever its result says.') makes the end of any session `work run` started a fault, not only a worker's. Reading A: the entry stays unchanged. Reading B (recommended): changed to '- **fault**: a failure outside a session's work that stops it: a push or git command failing, a worker changing a file under `.shop/work/` outside its stage directory, or a session `work run` started ending in an error, at its turn limit or without a result `work run` can read.'
- Glossary, term/stage and term/outcome: r129 to r132 speak of a round's and an answering session's 'stage' and 'stage's output', while the entry names only research, plan, implementation and review. Reading A: the entry stays and 'stage' in those lines is read loosely. Reading B (recommended): term/stage changed to '- **stage**: one session `work run` starts for a node from its status: research, plan, implementation and review by a worker, a round of a kind, or an answering session. Not: phase.' Similarly term/outcome ('the one word a worker's result gives') becomes 'the one word a session's result gives', r133 speaking of any session's result.

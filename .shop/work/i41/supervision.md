## Questions
- r147 and r132: r147 (added) reads: "**When** a round of the kind `apply` applies nothing because a capability-proposal's answers give what its drafts do not hold, `work run` shall take that as the round's output." r132 (existing, unchanged by these drafts) lists "an integrate or apply round's change list" as a stage's output, so an apply round's output would have two readings. Reading A (recommended): r147 stays and extends r132's list, r132 unchanged. Reading B: retire r132 and add a line reading "an integrate round's change list; an apply round's change list, or its finding that it applies nothing because the capability-proposal's answers give what its drafts do not hold", the rest as r132.
- r148 and r92: r148 (added) reads: "**When** a round of the kind `integrate` drafts a capability-proposal anew from its answers, `work run` shall write no second capability-proposal for that proposal's capability." r92 says an integrate round's change list gets a `drafted` capability-proposal for each capability it names. Reading A (recommended): r92 stays and r148 is the exception for a round started for a capability-proposal (r140). Reading B: retire r92 and add "**When** a round of the kind `integrate` that `work run` started for an initiative, not for a capability-proposal, ends with a change list, `work run` shall write a `drafted` capability-proposal for each capability the change list names, with the requirement ids and terms it adds, changes and removes on that capability."
- r142 gives two responses. r142 (added) reads: "**When** a round of the kind `integrate` drafts a capability-proposal anew from its answers, `work run` shall set the capability-proposal `drafted`, its earlier drafts kept in their round's directory." Reading A (recommended): split it; r142 becomes "... `work run` shall set the capability-proposal `drafted`." and a new line is added: "**When** a round of the kind `integrate` drafts a capability-proposal anew from its answers, `work run` shall keep the proposal's earlier drafts in their round's directory." Reading B: r142 stays as drafted.

# Requests and the context loop

## Lines
1. **When** `work run` is given a work-item every requirement of which is of a guarantees capability, `work run` shall deliver the work-item.
2. **Where** `--sessions <dir>` is given, `measure initiative` shall give each session's round trips, tool uses, dispatches and compactions.
3. **When** a session starts in a repository with an initiative that is neither `done` nor `abandoned`, the plugin's SessionStart hook shall give the session what `work status` prints.
4. **When** `work request` is run, `work` shall write a request whose asker and upstream each write their own part through `work`, in turns.
5. **When** every work-item of an initiative is `green` or `withdrawn` and every request it raised is `delivered`, `work` shall set the initiative to `done`.
6. **When** `work run` is started with no initiative, `work run` shall take every initiative that is neither `done` nor `abandoned` through its rounds, its drafting and its analysis.

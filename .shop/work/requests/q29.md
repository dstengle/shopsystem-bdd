# A backlog that carries session observations and rulings into requests

## Lines
1. The bounded context shall have a backlog that holds the observations and rulings of its sessions until a request holds them.
2. **When** the person gives a session a ruling or an observation outside an initiative, the session shall add it to the backlog in the same turn, with its date and the person's words.
3. **When** a session finds an observation outside an initiative, the session shall add it to the backlog in the same turn.
4. The session-start hook's text shall name each item of the backlog that no request holds.
5. **When** `work run` takes in an item of the backlog that no request holds, `work run` shall draft a request from it, naming the item as its source.
6. The session shall keep each ruling and observation of the bounded context in the repository.

## Questions
- term/backlog (added). Entry in full: "- **backlog**: the bounded context's ideas and observations that the person gives its sessions outside an initiative, kept in the repository, each with its date and the person's words." The word "observation" is already a record kind (`.shop/records/observations/`). Is an observation of the backlog such a record? Reading A (recommended): no; the entry gets a note that an observation of the backlog is not an observation record, and the lines stay as drafted. Reading B: yes; backlog r2 becomes "...add it to the backlog as an observation record in the same turn..." and the entry says so. term/log-of-temporary-rules (added: "- **log of temporary rules**: the bounded context's rules that the person gives its sessions outside an initiative, kept in the repository, each with its date and the person's words, until the person moves one to the backlog.") is asked nothing.

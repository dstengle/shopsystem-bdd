I've added b105 "Every noun is a kind of thing, so a verb whose frame takes thing takes a scenario" to the backlog and pushed it.

**The problem:** the glossary defines "thing" as anything a requirement names. But a noun only counts as a thing if its entry says `Kind of:` and that chain leads up to thing. Scenario has no `Kind of:`, so verbs that take a thing, like `give`, refuse it. That's why "shall give a scenario to the requirement" failed while I was shaping b103. Other nouns without `Kind of:` would hit the same wall.

The item's notes cover two ways to fix it:
- **Make thing the automatic root.** Every noun counts as a kind of thing without having to say so.
- **Give each noun a `Kind of:`.** Scenario and the other unattached nouns each get an explicit entry.

They also leave a separate glossary question open: whether a scenario should count as text as well, so that `write` could take it.

### the session, 2026-11-26T01:21:54.427000+00:00, bb4ce470-d25e-484c-9a4e-d53c3dd6778d

```text
It's a rule I added to this repository's batch settings (`[batch] rules` in `.shop/delivery.toml`), which every batch brief passes to its driver:

> The driver dispatches its task, fix and review subagents in the foreground (all of one step in one message, run_in_background false), so their results come back to it; it never ends its turn while one of its subagents is still running.

**Why it's needed:** when a driver dispatches a subagent with `run_in_background: true`, that subagent's completion notice doesn't reliably come back to the driver. Several times it came to me, the session that started the batch. The driver had already ended its turn to "wait", so it sat idle until I nudged it.
- **Batch-96:** idle for about an hour, costing $26 where a normal batch costs $8–12.
- **Batch-107:** the driver ended its turn while t2 was still running, but no prod was needed that time.
- **Batch-111:** idle until I restarted it just now.

**What the rule changes:** in the foreground, the driver's call blocks until the subagent replies, so the result lands back in the driver and it carries straight on. Putting several foreground calls in one message still runs the tasks in parallel, so nothing is lost by it.

**Why it's only partly working:** the rule lives in this repository's settings, and drivers still sometimes ignore it. b635 makes the plugin's own batch brief say it for every repository that uses the plugin, and I'd word it more firmly there. The `building-a-batch` skill's step 4 should say it too.
```

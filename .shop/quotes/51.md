### the session, 2026-11-18T14:37:34.093000+00:00, cc9fffad-6aad-4131-978b-bb1e37057b37

```text
You're right that it isn't just a compile. The compile itself took about 22 seconds; almost all of the wait was parsing the lines afterwards, run by `model parse` in-process.

**What happens when you add a word**

1. **Python writes the grammar.** `spec_rgl_vocab` reads every layer of the lexicon (the packs, the glossary, each capability's terms). From that, `spec_rgl` writes a GF grammar for this context. The generated `Context.gf` is about 730 KB, roughly 20,000 lines, because it has a function for every kind, frame, attribute, value, relation and relative clause. This takes a few seconds.
2. **GF compiles it.** `gf -make` builds it against the RGL into a 14 MB `Context.pgf`. That took 22 seconds (14:25:16 to 14:25:38). The result is cached in `~/.cache/shopsystem-bdd/pgf/<sha>/`, keyed by a hash of the generated sources.
3. **Every line is parsed again from scratch.** Parses are also cached on disk, but the cache key includes the grammar's hash. So one new term makes every earlier parse of that context a miss. This is why the same file against the unchanged spec took 33 seconds, and the reworded one took about 6½ minutes in total.
4. **Refused lines cost the most.** For a line that doesn't parse, `model` works out the hint ("parsed up to", "nearest words", "may follow"). It does that by asking GF for completions and re-parsing the prefix with each candidate. Ten of my 15 lines were refused, so most of the time went there.

**Two side effects worth knowing:**
- The cache has grown to 7.2 GB of compiled grammars (about 5,100 of them) plus 1.3 GB of parses, and nothing prunes it.
- Step 3 is likely the same cause as b340 ("work batch start's pick takes under five minutes when the ready items add terms").

**The parse result for decision-tiers:** 3 of the 15 reworded lines parsed cleanly (r19, r21, r29). The rest were refused, all for reasons I can fix:
- "the spec" has no antecedent. It needs a `This:` like `command` has.
- "has no tier" doesn't read because tier on […]
```

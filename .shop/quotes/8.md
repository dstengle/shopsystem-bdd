Agreed: those three should be refusals in the grammar, not left to the glossary or to whoever reads the line. I'll add this to the backlog now, since it's work you've stated.

I've added b82 "The grammar refuses a line with bad English: a verb missing its object or recipient, a condition missing its agent" to the backlog, and it's pushed to main. It covers all three problems:

1. **A verb missing its object.** The glossary entry for an event will say what kind of verb it is and what it takes, for example *ask for a video list*. The grammar then offers only that verb's form, so "a caller … asks" with nothing asked for is refused.
2. **A verb missing its recipient.** A verb declared with a recipient (*give X to a caller*) is refused without one. "Give a video" fails, and r1 and r2 end up phrased the same way.

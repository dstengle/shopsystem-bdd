### the session, 2026-11-27T18:42:40.595000+00:00, f74a063e-025b-47f1-82e6-17d669b251f6

```text
**What it's for:** an item's requirement lines stay on the item (`.shop/backlog/b<n>.md`) until its batch merges, and `spec/` on `main` is never edited directly. Laying is the moment an item's lines actually go into the spec: they're written into the batch branch's working copy, where they get built and tested. Taking them out again is "lifting".

**Where it sits:** it's a driven port of the batches service, its own area in the domain model (`spec/model/laying.md`, kind `port`). The port has four operations, in `scripts/laying.py`:

| Operation | What it does | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| lay | Writes an item's lines into the workspace's spec: requirements, model lines, terms, imports, moves, retirements, `serves:` lines, operation tables. Their ids go into `unbuilt:`. | `work batch start` |
| prepare | Run once all the batch's items are laid. It regenerates their scenarios and rewrites the spec corpus, the rule and construct catalogues, and the `## Composition` table. It also writes any decision a retirement supersedes. | `work batch start`, `work batch amend` |
| lift | Takes an item back out, restoring what the base held | `work batch park`, `work batch review` (for parked items), `work batch amend` (lift, then lay again) |
| check | Checks the laid item against the error-level rules, so a broken item goes back to `shaped` before the batch opens | `work batch start` |

**The adapter:** `laying_inprocess.py` does the work in-process, through the `backlog_*` modules' lay and unlay functions. Tests swap in a stand-in through `$SHOPSYSTEM_LAYING`.

The readiness bar (`work ready`) and `spec check --item` use the same lay mechanics on a throwaway copy of `spec/` to preview an item. They call those functions directly, not through the port.
```

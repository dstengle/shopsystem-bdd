## The scout comparison

On a copy of shopsystem-kb at `bd159cd^` with the batch plan removed, through `shopsystem-bdd:code-scout`, three runs a model for each dispatch. The commit changed 33 production and 36 test files that existed before it. The known conflict is the one that cost kb its tests: decisions 4 and 5 together leave a `kb:` reference dangling when a base type moves (`tests/test_a_type_moving_under_a_change.py`).

**Lands probe.**

| run | counted (production, test) | real files named | time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Haiku 1 | 31, 39 | 31 of 33, 36 of 36 | 174 s |
| Haiku 2 | 34, 41 | 30 of 33, 36 of 36 | 113 s |
| Haiku 3 | 29, 37 | 28 of 33, 34 of 36 | 79 s |
| Sonnet 1 | 31, 47 | 29 of 33, 36 of 36 | not recorded |
| Sonnet 2 | 32, 39 | 29 of 33, 35 of 36 | not recorded |
| Sonnet 3 | 32, 39 | 31 of 33, test names abbreviated in its notes | 59 s |

No gap. Every run puts the slice about four times over the budget. Every run misses `store.py` or `backups.py`, which the change reaches without spelling the form: the count is a floor.

**Task research**, on whether it names the dangling reference under "Tests the decisions break":

| | named it | production files named |
|---|---|---|
| Haiku | 1 of 3 (a second run named the file only as references to respell) | 32, 32, 32 of 33 |
| Sonnet | 2 of 3 | 33, 32, 30 of 33 |

With the two general-purpose runs that preceded the design (Haiku found it, Sonnet did not), each model found it in 2 of 4. Neither is reliable on it and neither is clearly better. Haiku stays the default. What this says about the design: the scout's list is a help to the planner and not a guarantee, and the implementer's stop on an unlisted test is the guard, which the `breaking` runs above show holding.

**Speed.** The first runs worked one command a turn: 12 to 23 turns for a probe, 32 to 45 for research, none with parallel calls, and 8 to 20 thousand characters of notes.

- Adding "Work in few turns" (searches run together, short notes) took a probe to 32 to 49 s in 4 to 5 turns and research to 63 to 102 s in 9 to 11 turns. But two of three probes then counted 8 and 10 test files of 36: the rule was read as "search less".
- Adding "never fewer searches", every spelling searched in every tree, and every file named: three probes counted 20 (with 15 more matched and flagged as not inspected), 31 and 30 production and 41, 38 and 43 test, naming 28 to 30 of 33 and 35 to 36 of 36, in 50 to 88 s and 6 to 8 turns.
- Research under the few-turns text: 49k to 79k tokens against 95k to 111k, and the dangling reference named in 2 of 3.

A research dispatch for "Tests the decisions break" alone, three Haiku runs: 51 to 98 s, the dangling reference named in 2 of 3. No better than the whole research, so a second, conflicts-only scout is not adopted.

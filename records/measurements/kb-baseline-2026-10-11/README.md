# shopsystem-kb batches 32–35: the baseline facts

The facts files `measure batch` wrote for shopsystem-kb's batches 32 to 35,
run on plugin 0.10.0 and 0.11.0. `kb-batch35-open.json` is the open batch at
the time of measuring. Read them with `scripts/measure show <file>`; set two
side by side with `scripts/measure compare`.

The figures the decisions cite (Lands exceeded in 15 of 23 slices, 7 of 17
per-task reviews clean, implementers on Opus 32 of 42 times, the
controller's share of cache reads) are from these files, as tabulated in
`records/review-findings/2026-10-13-kb-and-bdd-assessment.md`.

# Construct catalogue

Generated by `model constructs --write` from `scripts/model_constructs_table.py` and grammar 4 compiled in the catalogue's context, `grammar/corpus/constructs/`; do not edit. `model constructs --check` says whether it is current. A requirement line about the language names a construct by its code. Each example is parsed to one tree that uses its construct and linearises back word for word, and its reading is its logical form (`model parse --form`); `tests/scripts/test_model_constructs.py` reads each example again as part of the acceptance corpus.

Words of a context, not constructs: `k_\w+_n|a_\w+|t_[a-z0-9_]+_\d+_v_\w+|Many\d+|Count_\d+|CodeLit`.

The joints of every line, not constructs: `k_\w+_(?:UseN|Det|One|Simple|Up_k_\w+|OneAct)|OneCond|OneResp|NoPre|PPos`.

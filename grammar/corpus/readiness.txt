# readiness: sentences that parse with exactly one tree in the fixture glossary's grammar.
While an initiative is `started` and the initiative is complete, when the person runs `work ready` on the initiative, `work` shall set the status of the initiative to `done`.
While an initiative is `started` and the initiative is not complete, if the person runs `work ready` on the initiative, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the reason and change nothing.
If the person runs `work ready` on a request that is not `open`, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the status of the request and change nothing.
When the person runs `work ready` on a request, `work` shall not ask a question.
When the person runs `work ready` on an initiative that is `proposed`, `work` shall set the status of the initiative to `started`.
If the person runs `work ready` on an initiative that is not `proposed`, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the initiative and change nothing.
When the person runs `work ready` on a work-item, `work` shall print the status of the work-item.
Where `--json` is given, when the person runs `work ready` on a request, `work` shall print a JSON object.

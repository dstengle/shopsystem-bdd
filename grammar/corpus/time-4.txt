# a moment within the last duration, in digits or in words, the unit agreeing with the number; and negated
While a deadline of a stage is within the last 7 days, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the stage.
While a deadline of a stage is within the last one day, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the stage.
While a deadline of a stage is not within the last twelve hours, when a session ends the stage, `work` shall print the stage.
# a moment after or before a code or another moment
While a deadline of a stage is after `2026-01-01` and the deadline is before `2027-01-01`, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the stage.
While a deadline of a stage is not after a deadline of a stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
# the clock as a trigger: "the clock" is the run's own clock (This:), and a schedule is a moment
When the clock reaches a deadline of a stage, `work` shall print the stage.
When the clock reaches a schedule, `work` shall print every stage.
# the perfect of an action verb as history, its subject in either number, and since a moment
While a session has ended a stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the stage.
While sessions have written a file, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the file.
While a session has written no file since `2026-01-01`, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
# the newest and the oldest of a kind
When the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the newest stage and not print the oldest stage.
# a number with no unit where a duration is due; a negated history with "a K" (R4); a state verb in the perfect
While a deadline of a stage is within the last 7, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the stage.

While a work-item is `ready`, when the person runs `work batch start`, `work` shall write an initiative that is `started` and set the status of the work-item to `claimed`.
While a work-item is `ready` and the work-item is not `green`, when the person runs `work batch start`, `work` shall set the status of the work-item to `drafted`.
When the person runs `work batch status` on an initiative, `work` shall print every work-item and the status of the work-item.
While an initiative is `started`, when the person runs `work batch resume` on the initiative, `work` shall not ask a question.
While an initiative is `started`, when the person runs `work batch review` on the initiative, `work` shall push a commit.
When the person runs `work batch abandon` on an initiative that is `started`, `work` shall set the status of the initiative to `abandoned`.
If the person runs `work batch abandon` on an initiative that is not `started`, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the status of the initiative and change nothing.
Where `--json` is given, when the person runs `work batch status` on an initiative, `work` shall print a JSON object.

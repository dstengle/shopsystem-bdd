When `features list` is run, `features` shall read every feature.
Where `--json` is given, `features list` shall print one JSON object.
When `features count` is run, `features` shall print the number of scenarios.
If the person runs `features bindings` on a scenario in no feature, then `features` shall print the scenario.
`features` shall not change a feature.
When the person runs `features generate`, `features` shall generate every feature.
When `features generate` is run, `features` shall commit every feature and push the commit.
Where `--check` is given, `features` shall change nothing.
When `features deprecated` is run, `features` shall list every scenario in the feature `work-queue`.

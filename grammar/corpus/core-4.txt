# core-4: the grammar 4 twin of core.txt (the CNL 4 design, I7a): one line for each construct of the base grammar,
# parsed in the smallest codified-2 context (tests/scripts/fixtures/codified-2/: the CLI pack and a few words), each
# with one tree that linearises back word for word; or a line followed by "= refused: <reason>", which grammar 4
# refuses with that reason. Later grammar items add their constructs and refusals here.

# When, a trigger whose verb agrees with a singular subject, and a response of the CLI pack
When the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
When a session writes a file, `work` shall print the file.
# agreement from the RGL: a plural subject (b113)
When sessions write a file, `work` shall print the file.
While sessions follow a stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall end the stage.
When sessions writes a file, `work` shall print the file.
= refused: the verb write does not agree in number with its subject
When a session write a file, `work` shall print the file.
= refused: the verb write does not agree in number with its subject
While a session follow a stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall end the stage.
= refused: the verb follow does not agree in number with its subject
# If, then: two acts under one shall, an act with fixed words and a kind in its frame, and "nothing"
If `work` refuses a command, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the reason and change nothing.
# three acts, the last negated: A, B and not C; a frame with a preposition before its second kind
When the person runs `work start` on a stage, `work` shall end the stage, print the stage and not write a file.
# shall not
When a session ends a stage, `work` shall not change the stage.
# While with polarity: a state verb negated ("does not"), an attribute's value, and, or
While a session does not follow a stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
While a stage is not `done` and sessions follow the stage, when a session ends the stage, `work` shall print the stage.
While a stage is `open` or a stage is `done`, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
# two responses whose subjects differ
When a session ends a stage, `work` shall exit successfully, and the person shall write a file.
# Where, and Where with While; a line with no trigger
Where a stage is `open`, when a session writes a file, `work` shall print every file.
Where a stage is `open`, while sessions follow the stage, if a session ends the stage, then `work` shall exit with an error naming the stage.
`work` shall not change `spec/index.md`.
# determiners: a, the, every, a bare plural, the plural
When the person runs `work start`, `work` shall print the files.
# an option in the trigger (b134): a code before its noun, "the `--json` option", or the code alone
When the person runs `work backlog` with the `--json` option, `work` shall print the output.
When a session runs `work backlog` with `--json`, `work` shall exit successfully.
# a noun of the wrong kind for its slot is not a grammar 4 line
When a session writes a stage, `work` shall print the stage.
= refused: does not parse at stage

When a session follows a stage, `work` shall print the stage.
= refused: the verb follow is a state, and a trigger takes an action
If sessions follow a stage, then `work` shall exit successfully.
= refused: the verb follow is a state, and a trigger takes an action
While a session writes a file, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.
= refused: the verb write is an action, and a While clause takes a state
While a stage is `open` and a session does not end the stage, when the person runs `work start`, `work` shall exit successfully.

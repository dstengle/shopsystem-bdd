# design-model-check: sentences that parse with exactly one tree in the fixture glossary's grammar.
When `model check` is run, `model` shall print every violation.
Where `--json` is given, `model` shall print one JSON object.
When the person runs `model why` on a requirement, `model` shall print the requirement and every capability-proposal that adds the requirement.
If the person runs `model check` on a requirement in no capability, then `model` shall exit with an error naming the requirement.
`model` shall not write a file.
When `model check` is run, `model` shall read every design-decision that is `accepted`.
When the person runs `model parse`, `model` shall print "parsed" and the number of requirements.
When `model lexicon` is run, `model` shall list every term.
When the person runs `model check` on a capability-proposal that argues no capability, `model` shall print the capability-proposal.

# Rule catalogue

Generated by `model rules --write` from `scripts/model.lp` and the rules of `model lexicon --check`; do not edit. `model rules --check` says whether it is current. A requirement line names a rule by its code.

Each entry says its purpose, the item it came by (Since:) and the items that changed it (Changed:); Since: and Changed: name items of `missingmass.io/shopsystem/shopsystem-bdd`, kept in `grammar/catalogue-entries.toml`.

- Purpose: A job's needs never close a cycle, so its trace joins the traces of the jobs it needs once.
- Since: b643
- Severity: error
- Implemented in: `scripts/model.lp`
- Cited by: no line
- Fails on:

  ```prolog
  pjob("j").
  pjob("n").
  pneeds("j","n").
  pneeds("n","j").
  ```

- Passes on:

  ```prolog
  pjob("j").
  pjob("n").
  pneeds("j","n").
  ```

## v_t8

- Purpose: Relations live in one table, so the grammar and checks read every relation the same way.

- Cited by: no line
- Fails on:

  ```markdown

  ```

- Passes on:

  ```markdown

  - **thing** (noun): A thing.

  ## Relations

  | from | word | to | how many |
  |---|---|---|---|

  ```
